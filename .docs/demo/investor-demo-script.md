# Investor Demo Script — Recorded (4 min)

> **Goal:** Get them to book a call. Not close a deal. Not explain everything.
> **Format:** Screen recording with voiceover (record screen silently, add voice after)
> **Rule:** Speed up all loading/thinking in post. Nobody watches a spinner.

---

## ACT 1: THE HOOK (0:00–0:25)

**Screen:** Black or simple Vibey logo. No product yet.

> "Hey, my name is Sefi — co-founder and CTO of Vibey. I ran a company with 82 employees across 20+ SaaS tools. Vibey replaces all of it."

_Beat._

> "Vibey lets you hire an AI workforce. Not one assistant — a CEO who manages a team of specialized agents, each with their own brain, their own skills, and access to 35+ integrations."

**What this does:** Problem (82 employees, 20+ tools) → Frame (AI workforce, not a chatbot) in 25 seconds. Investor now has a lens for everything that follows.

**What NOT to say here:**

- Don't list features (skills, video editing, image generation...)
- Don't explain brains yet
- Don't mention Telegram/Slack/personal vs org accounts
- Don't say "this was a painful problem and Vibey is the solution for this painful problem"

---

## ACT 2: THE ONE FLOW (0:25–2:30)

**Screen:** Open Vibey. Show the platform briefly (2-3 seconds), then go straight into a conversation.

### What to show:

One conversation where you tell the CEO to launch a marketing campaign for a real brand. The CEO analyzes the brief, delegates to agents (copywriter, designer, strategist), and delivers outputs.

### Narration guide:

**0:25 — Open the conversation**

> "Watch — I'm going to ask the CEO to build a full marketing campaign for [brand name]."

_Type or show the message. Keep it natural, one sentence._

**0:40 — CEO starts working**

> "The CEO breaks down the brief, figures out what's needed, and starts delegating to the right agents."

_Show the mission being created, subtasks appearing. Speed this up in post if needed._

**1:00 — Agents working (this is the money sequence)**

> "The copywriter is pulling from the brand brain — voice, audience, offers — all learned from previous conversations. I never fed it a brief for this. It already knows."

_This is where the brain moat lands. ONE line. Don't explain the three layers. Just: "it already knows."_

**1:30 — Outputs arriving**

> "Landing page copy. Ad variations. Social content. All on-brand, all from one conversation."

_Show deliverables appearing. If a funnel page is generated, show it._

**2:00 — The publish moment**

> "And now — one click. It's live."

_Show the funnel or content publishing. Cut to a browser tab showing the live page on a real domain. THIS is the last visual of Act 2. The output is real, not a mockup._

### What NOT to do in Act 2:

- Don't pause to explain multi-agent architecture
- Don't show the agent marketplace or recruiter
- Don't explain user brain vs agent brain vs campaign brain
- Don't show Telegram/Slack integration
- Don't say "you can also..." for anything

---

## ACT 3: BRIAN — SOCIAL PROOF (2:30–3:15)

**Screen:** Can stay on the platform or cut to a simple visual. Doesn't need heavy screen action — the story carries this.

> "This isn't a prototype. One of our enterprise clients — Brian, 640K subscribers, seven-figure monthly revenue — used Vibey to recruit a custom financial analyst agent. It connected to four of his payment platforms and generated a multi-year financial report: churn rates, cost per client, growth per program. No human analyst. One conversation."

**What this does:**

- Proves it's not vaporware (real client, real numbers)
- Proves it's not just marketing/funnels (financial analysis = horizontal platform)
- The word "recruit" implies the system creates custom agents on demand (don't explain the recruiter — let the implication land)

### What NOT to say:

- Don't explain how the recruiter agent works
- Don't list other use cases ("you can also do operations, customer service, finance...")
- One story. Let it breathe.

---

## ACT 4: THE MOAT (3:15–3:45)

**Screen:** Subtle — maybe the brain/knowledge panel, or just the conversation thread. This is voiceover-driven.

> "Every conversation makes the brain smarter. The user brain learns you. The agent brain learns its craft. The campaign brain learns the project."

_Pause._

> "After 30 days, your Vibey organization knows more about your business than any new hire ever could. That knowledge compounds — and it never leaves."

**What this does:** Plants the moat without a whiteboard session. Three sentences. The investor now understands: this gets better over time, and the switching cost is real.

---

## ACT 5: THE CLOSE (3:45–4:00)

**Screen:** Vibey logo or the dashboard. Clean.

> "We're live. We have enterprise clients. And we're looking for partners who understand that the AI workforce isn't coming — it's here."

> "Let's talk."

**End card:** Contact info / calendar link.

## CHEAT SHEET

### Timing breakdown

| Act         | Time      | Purpose                                                           |
| ----------- | --------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Hook     | 0:00–0:25 | Problem + frame                                                   |
| 2. One Flow | 0:25–2:30 | The "holy shit" — one conversation, full campaign, published live |
| 3. Brian    | 2:30–3:15 | Social proof + horizontal capability                              |
| 4. Moat     | 3:15–3:45 | Why it compounds (three brains, 30 seconds)                       |
| 5. Close    | 3:45–4:00 | CTA                                                               |

### Things that are BANNED from this demo

These are all real and impressive. They belong in the follow-up call, NOT this video.

- Agent marketplace walkthrough
- Recruiter agent explanation
- Personal vs organization account types
- Telegram/Slack integration
- Onboarding flow
- Skills/integrations list
- How the brain architecture works in detail
- Agent-to-agent communication explanation
- "You can also..."

### The one-line test

After watching, the investor should be able to say:

> "It's an AI workforce platform — you talk to a CEO agent, it delegates to a team, and they produce real marketing campaigns and financial reports from one conversation. The brain learns your business over time."

If they can't say that, the demo tried to show too much.

### Recording tips

1. **Record screen silently first.** Get the flow perfect. Redo it until the outputs look great.
2. **Add voiceover after.** This lets you control pacing and speak with confidence instead of multitasking.
3. **Speed up all AI thinking/loading.** 15-second waits become 2-second waits. The feeling of speed IS the product.
4. **End on the live page.** The last product visual = the published output on a real domain. Not the dashboard.
5. **Total runtime: 3:30–4:30.** Under 4 is ideal. Over 5 is too long. If you're at 6 minutes, cut — don't speed-talk.

### Practice reps

**Rep 1:** Read the script out loud with no screen. Just practice the voiceover flow. Does it feel natural? Where do you stumble?

**Rep 2:** Record the screen flow without talking. Get the clicks and outputs dialed in. Where does it lag? What needs to be sped up?

**Rep 3:** Combine. Record screen + voiceover together (even if you'll re-record the voice later). Find where the narration and the screen don't sync.

**Rep 4:** Watch it back as if you're an investor who's seen 50 AI demos this month. What makes you close the tab? Cut that part.

**Rep 5:** Final take. Ship it.
