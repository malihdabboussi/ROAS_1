02 — Skills

The 8 Skills

Each skill is a set of specific instructions, frameworks, and processes that an agent uses to do its job. Here's what each one does and why it matters.

brand-north-star ON VERA

Brand North Star

The foundation everything else builds on. This encodes your brand identity as rules, not vibes, so every piece of content passes through the same filter.

What's Inside

  • Voice DNA — Your Entertainer-Strategist archetype, 4 tone registers (Comedic, Inspiring, Educational, Promotional), sentence patterns, vocabulary rules, and 18 anti-slop filters that catch generic AI-sounding output
  • Content Pillars + Ratios — 5 pillars (Humor, Motivation, Promotion, BTS, Authority) each targeting 20% of monthly output. Auto-flags when any pillar drifts below 15% or above 30%
  • Framework Library — SRS (8 archetypes), STD Method, 5P Relatability Engine, Shareability Framework (5 DM Triggers), 6 Viral Hook Styles, Hormozi's Hook-Retain-Reward
  • Tags Taxonomy — Difficulty (easy/medium/hard/airplane/location), funnel position (TOF/BOFU), urgency (none/scheduled/brand-deal), platform hints
How it works Idea: "POV: Your algorithm coach finds out you're still posting at peak hours" Pillar: Authority (17% this month — below 20% target, FILL) Framework: SRS Pattern Interrupt + Comedic register DM Triggers: "This is so me" + "Makes me look smart for sharing" Difficulty: easy (single setup, no props) Score: HIGH PRIORITY (fills pillar gap + hits 2 triggers)
content-ideation ON VERA

Content Ideation

Generates, scores, and manages your content idea pipeline. Every idea gets ICE-scored and tagged before it reaches you.

4 Idea Generation Methods

Method Trigger What Happens
Inspiration Adaptation You send a video or link Extracts the structure (not the topic), maps to an SRS archetype, rewrites in your voice
Pillar Gap Filling Agent-initiated Checks 30-day pillar ratios, generates 3-5 ideas for underrepresented pillars
Brand Deal Pre-Planning Calendar-driven Works backwards from deal date (script 14 days, film 10, edit 7) and generates natural integration ideas
5-Question Extraction You drop a raw topic Extracts 5+ angles: teach, observe, contrarian, deeper story, listicle. One idea becomes five.

ICE Scoring

Impact (1-10) × Confidence (1-10) × Ease (1-10) = Score. Above 500 = prioritize. Below 200 = backlog.

filming-operations ON JETT

Filming Operations

Manages the logistics between "script approved" and "footage uploaded." Your content is planned and batched, not daily vlogging, so this skill handles slates, folders, checklists, and buffer tracking.

Key Processes

  • Weekly Slate (Monday 8am) — Pulls up to 20 ready-to-film pieces, balanced across pillars, difficulty, and funnel position. Groups similar setups on the same day. Presents for your approval.
  • Pre-Shoot Checklist — Script link, shot list, props, location, Google Drive folder (RAW/ and EDIT/ subfolders) all verified before filming day.
  • Post-Film Protocol — You mark "filmed," agent pings you to upload to the Drive link, monitors the RAW folder, auto-advances status when footage lands.
  • Buffer Tracking — Maintains a 2-week buffer. Alerts you when it drops below 1 week. Critical alert below 4 pieces.

repurposing-engine ON RIO

Repurposing Engine

Takes your winning content and multiplies it across channels. One piece becomes 5-10 derivatives. You approve the batch, they get scheduled.

The 3-Level Waterfall

  • Level 1 — Format Swaps: Winning reel → carousel, winning carousel → reel script, newsletter → Twitter thread
  • Level 2 — Angle Extraction (Justin Welsh): From one idea, generate a story, listicle, teardown, observation, contrarian take, past vs present, and prediction. Pick 2-3 that fit naturally.
  • Level 3 — Channel Adaptation: Twitter/X (280-char standalone tweets), LinkedIn (framework posts), YouTube (5-15 min expanded scripts), Email (CTA snippets)

Top performers 3+ months old get flagged for long-tail re-spins — your audience has grown since the original, new followers haven't seen it.

04 — Getting Started

What You Can Do Now

Your agents are live. Here's exactly how to start using them.

Try These Commands

Open Vibey and talk to your team. These workflows trigger automatically.

  • content — Start the full Content OS pipeline. Drop an idea, link, or say "fill gaps"
  • newsletter — Kick off this week's newsletter research and draft
  • repurpose — Take a top performer and generate 5-10 channel derivatives

Things to Try First

  • Drop an inspiration link. Send Vera a reel you liked. She'll extract the structure, rewrite it in your voice, score it, and present a tagged script.
  • Say "fill gaps." Vera checks your pillar ratios and generates ideas for any pillar that's underrepresented. Ranked by ICE score.
  • Approve a script. When you approve, Jett auto-creates the Google Drive folder, builds the pre-shoot checklist, and queues it for the next filming slate.
  • Give feedback on an edit. Say something like "it's shaky at the start and too slow." Tessa translates that into timestamped editor notes and sends to the right person via Notion.
  • Ask "how are we doing." Rio pulls your monthly pillar report, top performers, and recommendations.

The Coaching Loop

Every time you revise a script, the system learns. Your edits are tracked as coaching data. Over time, the scripts get closer to your voice with fewer revisions needed. The more you use it, the smarter it gets.
