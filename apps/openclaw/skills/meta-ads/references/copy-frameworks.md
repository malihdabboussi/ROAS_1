# Ad Copy Frameworks

## Hook Formulas

### 1. Pain-Agitate-Solve (PAS)

- **Pain:** Name the problem clearly
- **Agitate:** Twist the knife — make them feel it
- **Solve:** Present your offer as the relief

### 2. Before-After-Bridge (BAB)

- **Before:** Current painful state
- **After:** Desired transformed state
- **Bridge:** Your offer connects the two

### 3. Problem-Promise-Proof-Push (4P)

- **Problem:** What they're struggling with
- **Promise:** What you'll deliver
- **Proof:** Why they should believe you (social proof, stats, credentials)
- **Push:** CTA with urgency or incentive

### 4. AIDA

- **Attention:** Pattern-interrupt hook
- **Interest:** Relevant detail that resonates
- **Desire:** Paint the transformation
- **Action:** Clear CTA

### 5. Story-Based

- **Scene:** Set the relatable moment
- **Struggle:** Show the tension
- **Solution:** Reveal the path forward
- **Step:** Tell them what to do next

## Hook Types (First Line)

The first 125 characters decide if they read or scroll. Use one of these:

1. **Question hook:** "Still doing X the hard way?"
2. **Bold claim:** "X changed everything about how I Y."
3. **Contrarian:** "Everyone says X. They're wrong."
4. **Callout:** "Hey [specific person/role] — this is for you."
5. **Story opener:** "Last Tuesday at 3AM, I realized something."
6. **Stat shock:** "87% of [group] fail at X. Here's why."
7. **If-then:** "If you're [situation], you need to see this."
8. **Confession:** "I wasted $X on Y before I found Z."

## Primary Text Structure

```
[Hook — first 125 chars, pattern interrupt]

[Agitation / Story — 2-3 short lines expanding the pain]

[Pivot — transition to solution]

[Value prop — what they get, why it matters]

[CTA — emoji + clear action + benefit]
```

### Formatting Rules

- Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences max)
- Line breaks between sections for readability
- Use "you" language (not "we" or "our")
- One emoji per CTA line max; avoid emoji soup
- Front-load benefit in headline (not brand name)

## Headline Best Practices

- Lead with benefit or transformation
- Use numbers when possible ("5 Steps to X")
- Match the hook's emotional register
- Avoid clickbait that the landing page can't deliver on

## CTA Best Practices

- Match CTA button to funnel stage (Download for lead magnets, Learn More for awareness)
- Create urgency without being sleazy (scarcity, timeliness, relevance)
- Tell them exactly what happens next ("Download the free framework")

## Avatar-Driven Copy

When an avatar/persona is provided, adapt copy by:

1. **Language:** Mirror the words they use to describe their problems
2. **Scenarios:** Reference their specific daily frustrations
3. **Aspirations:** Speak to their desired transformation
4. **Objections:** Preemptively address why they haven't solved this yet
5. **Identity:** Speak to who they see themselves as (not who they are)
