**Vibey Mini** is the Vibey Chrome extension. It opens a sidebar next to any tab so everything you do with Vibey in the main app (chatting, saving to your Brain, checking missions) is one click away, without switching tabs.

## Four Things Live Inside

| Tab | What you can do |
|---|---|
| **Chat** | Start a streaming conversation with any of your agents. Attach the page you're reading as context in one click. |
| **Brain** | Send the current page, a text selection, or a readable article into your [User Brain](/brain/user-brain), an [Agent Brain](/brain/agent-brain), a [Company Brain](/brain/company-brain), or a [Customer Brain](/brain/customer-brain). |
| **Missions** | See every active mission with status and assigned agent. Click through to the mission detail in its Space. |
| **Sessions** | Approve, disable, refresh, or forget your signed-in sessions for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, YouTube, and Reddit, so agents can act on those platforms as you. |

## It Also Works When You're Not Looking

Even with the sidebar closed, Vibey Mini does three quiet things in the background:

- **Right-click any page or selection** → save to your Brain without opening the sidebar.
- **Capture sessions on approved sites** once you've enabled session sharing. You'll see sites appear in the Sessions tab seconds after you visit them.
- **Flag expiring sessions** on the extension icon. A red dot on the toolbar icon means something needs your attention.

## What It Doesn't Do

- It **does not browse the web for you.** Your agents do that in [their own secure environment](/talents/web-browsing). Vibey Mini just saves your sessions so they can act as you.
- It **does not capture pages automatically.** Brain captures are always deliberate. One click, one tab, one save.
- It **does not touch sites that are not on the approved list.** Session capture only runs for the platforms your agents need.
