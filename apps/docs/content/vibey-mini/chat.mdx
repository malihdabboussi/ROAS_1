The **Chat** tab in Vibey Mini is a full streaming conversation with your Vibey agents. The same chat as the main app, living in your browser sidebar.

## What You Can Do

- **Continue any conversation.** Your existing chats from the main app show up in the dropdown at the top. Pick one, read the history, keep going.
- **Start a fresh conversation.** Hit the `+` button in the header to start a new one.
- **Attach the page you are on.** A toggle in the composer adds the current page's URL, title, selection, and article text to your next message, so you do not have to paste links or quotes.
- **Jump to the main app.** The arrow icon in the header opens the conversation in the main Vibey window where the full Space (Docs, Channels, Views) is available.

## When to Use Each Tab

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| You're reading an article and want your agent's take | Open Chat, toggle the page-context switch on, ask a question |
| You need to continue a thread from earlier | Open the dropdown, pick the conversation |
| You want to brainstorm without referencing the page | Toggle page-context off |
| The chat is getting long, you want a bigger window | Click the open-in-app arrow to jump to the main Vibey window |

## The Page-Context Toggle

When the toggle is **on**, each message you send includes:

- The URL of the current tab
- The page title
- Any text you've highlighted
- The cleaned article text (if the page has one. Extracted the same way a reader mode does)

The agent sees it as context, so you can just say *"summarize this"* or *"turn this into 3 LinkedIn posts"* without pasting.

When the toggle is **off**, your messages are pure. No page data attached.

<Callout type="tip">
  Highlight the specific paragraph you care about before sending. The agent prioritizes your selection over the full article.
</Callout>

## Streaming and Tool Events

The agent's reply streams live. Same as the main app. When an agent uses a tool (searching, generating an image, pulling data from the web), you will see a little chip appear inline:

- **Purple/blue chip** tool starting
- **Green chip** finished successfully
- **Red chip** something failed

Tools show their name and label so you know what the agent is doing.

## Organizations and Campaigns

If your active [organization](/organization/how-organizations-work) changes, chats follow automatically. The sidebar uses whichever org you last selected in the main app.

Conversations tied to a specific [campaign](/campaigns/organizing-with-campaigns) stay in that campaign. When you start a new chat from Vibey Mini, it goes into your default scope unless you pick a campaign.

## Limits

- **Mobile-shaped.** The sidebar is narrow. For heavy conversations with lots of artifacts, click the open-in-app arrow to jump to the main Vibey window.
- **One session at a time.** If you start a new conversation while another is streaming, the streaming response is cancelled first.
- **Tool-produced artifacts** (generated images, documents, videos) show up in the main app's Space (Docs view, Media view, or the matching artifact view). The sidebar does not render large artifacts inline.
