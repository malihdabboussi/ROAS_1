---

The **Missions** tab in Vibey Mini gives you a live snapshot of everything your team is working on, without switching tabs or opening the main app.

## What You See

Every mission that is actively in progress shows up as a card with:

| Shown | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Title** | What the mission is about |
| **Status chip** | Where the mission is in its lifecycle |
| **Assigned agent** | Which agent is currently handling it |
| **Last updated** | When the mission state last changed |

Missions are filtered to just the active statuses. Things that are moving or waiting on you. Completed, cancelled, and archived missions do not appear here (see them in the Missions view inside the relevant Space).

## Active Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Inbox** | New, not yet picked up |
| **Planning** | Vibey is breaking it down into subtasks |
| **Todo** | Ready to start |
| **In Progress** | Agent is actively working |
| **Review** | Done, waiting for your OK |
| **Blocked** | Stuck on something. Usually needs your input |
| **Pending Approval** | Action ready to fire, waiting for your approval |

## What You Can Do

- **Refresh the list** click **Refresh** in the top right to re-check
- **Click a mission** opens the mission detail in its Space in a new tab
- **Open the main app** the button at the bottom opens Home

## When This Is Useful

- You are browsing and want to **peek at what your team is doing** without leaving the tab
- An agent is **stuck on a mission** and you want to check in
- You just fired off a mission and want to **watch its status move**
- You are reviewing **how many things are in the Review column** to plan your day

## What You Will Not See Here

- Full mission detail (subtasks, logs, deliverables). Open the mission inside its Space for those
- Cancelled or completed missions. The sidebar focuses on active work
- Mission creation. Start new missions from Home or from Chat in the sidebar

<Callout type="tip">
  Use Missions in the sidebar as a **status glance**, not a management tool. For approvals, retries, or changing mission settings, click through to the mission's Space.
</Callout>

## Organizations

If you are part of multiple organizations, the Missions list shows missions in your **currently active org**. Switch orgs in the main app and the sidebar follows.
