When you stumble on something worth keeping (an article, a quote, a reference link), Vibey Mini sends it straight into your [Brain](/brain/how-the-brain-works). No copy-paste, no upload step.

## Three Ways to Save

| Entry point | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Brain tab in the sidebar** | When you want control over scope, target brain, and what gets saved |
| **Right-click menu** | When you just want to save the current page or a selection without opening the sidebar |

## Using the Brain Tab

Open the sidebar, switch to the **Brain** tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick your account">
    If you're part of an organization, choose which workspace the saved content belongs to. Personal accounts skip this.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the target brain">
    Five options:
    - **User Brain** personal context available to all your agents
    - **Company Brain** how the org operates, available to every org agent
    - **Customer Brain** real signals from contacts and customer interactions
    - **Agent Brain** expertise for one specific agent (shown by agent name)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what to capture">
    Three modes:
    - **Link** just the URL and title
    - **Selection** whatever text you have highlighted on the page
    - **Article** the cleaned, readable article text (works on blog posts, news, docs, anything with a readable main body)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send to brain queue">
    Click **Send to brain queue**. The capture is queued and processed in the background. You will see it appear in your Brain within a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using the Right-Click Menu

Right-click anywhere on a page to see two options:

| Menu item | Appears when | What it sends |
|---|---|---|
| **Send selection to your Brain** | You have highlighted some text | Just your selection, scoped to the brain you last used in the sidebar |
| **Send page link to your Brain** | Right-clicking the page or frame | The page URL and title |

The capture goes to the brain you **last selected** in the sidebar. If you have not set one, it defaults to **User Brain**.

<Callout type="tip">
  Want to save a specific quote to a specific Agent Brain? Open the sidebar first, switch to that agent's brain in the Brain tab, then go back to the page and right-click. The selection goes to that target.
</Callout>

## The Three Capture Modes (When to Use Each)

| Mode | Example use |
|---|---|
| **Link** | Quickly bookmark a reference. "This competitor's pricing page is worth remembering" |
| **Selection** | Capture a specific quote. "This one paragraph about their ICP is gold" |
| **Article** | Save the whole readable article for your agents to reference. "Read this long-form interview and save it to my Brain" |

## What Your Agents Do With It

Everything you save is indexed for semantic search. When an agent works on a relevant task, they find what you have saved automatically:

- A [copywriter](/team/meet-your-agents) writing for a campaign pulls from the campaign and Space context
- Any agent answering a question about you pulls from your [User Brain](/brain/user-brain)
- Customer-facing agents pull tagged customer signals from your [Customer Brain](/brain/customer-brain)
- A specialist working in their domain pulls from their own [Agent Brain](/brain/agent-brain)
- Every org agent reads from the [Company Brain](/brain/company-brain) for org context

See [How the Brain Works](/brain/how-the-brain-works) for the full picture.

## Checking the Queue

After a send, the capture goes through processing: fetching, cleaning, chunking, embedding. This takes seconds to a few minutes depending on length.

The main app shows a full import-jobs queue in the Brain settings. Vibey Mini focuses on fast capture. To check or troubleshoot a specific save, jump to the main app.

## Limits

- **200 captures per session, soft cap.** Vibey Mini throttles repeated captures of the same URL to avoid duplicates.
- **Very long articles** get processed the same way. No hard length limit, but very large captures take longer to appear.
- **PDFs and non-HTML content** are not captured by the sidebar. Upload those directly in the main app's Brain settings.
- **Private pages behind logins** capture the HTML as it appears to you. If a page redirects you to a login, only the login page is captured.
