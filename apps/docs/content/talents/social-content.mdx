## What You Get

Vibey creates social posts with copy, images, hashtags, and scheduling - and publishes them directly to **LinkedIn** and **Instagram** through connected integrations.

## Supported Post Types

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Text Only** | Copy-only posts, typically for LinkedIn |
| **Single Image** | One image with caption |
| **Carousel** | Multi-slide image posts with individual captions per slide |
| **Story** | Vertical format for Instagram Stories |
| **Reel** | Short-form vertical video for Instagram Reels |

## Publishing Platforms

| Platform | What Vibey can do |
|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Create and publish posts directly to your profile |
| **Instagram** | Create media containers and publish posts, carousels, stories, and reels |

Connect both platforms in **Workspace Settings → [Integrations](/integrations/setting-up-integrations)** to enable direct publishing.

## How to Create Social Content

In Team chat, describe what you need:

- "Create a LinkedIn post about our new offer"
- "Write an Instagram carousel breaking down our 5-step process"
- "Turn this blog article into 3 LinkedIn posts"
- "Create a week of social content from this video transcript"
- "Make an Instagram reel script for our product launch"

Vibey uses your [offer](/talents/offers), [buyer persona](/talents/avatars), and campaign/Space context to write posts that match your audience and tone.

## Scheduling

Posts can be scheduled for a specific date and time. Once scheduled, they appear in the campaign's schedule view where you can see what's going out, when, and on which platform. Posts can be unscheduled if plans change.

The lifecycle of a social post:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being written or edited |
| **Ready** | Approved and waiting to be scheduled or published |
| **Scheduled** | Set to publish at a specific time |
| **Published** | Live on the platform |
| **Failed** | Publish attempt failed - check integration connection |

## Research Tools

Your agents can also research content from other platforms to inform your posts:

- Pull profile data and content from Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, Twitter, and Facebook
- Transcribe video content from URLs
- Analyze competitor posts and engagement patterns

This research feeds into better content creation - your agents study what works before writing.

## Training for Your Voice

The first round of posts might not sound exactly like you. Refine it:

1. Review the output and note what matches your voice and what doesn't
2. Give feedback: "We don't use emojis on LinkedIn" or "Our hooks always start with a question"
3. Save the refined approach as a [skill](/team/agent-skills) so the agent nails it consistently going forward

<Callout type="tip">
  Upload your best-performing posts to the [agent's Brain](/brain/agent-brain). When the agent sees what "great" looks like for your audience, output quality jumps.
</Callout>
