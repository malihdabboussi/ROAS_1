## What You Get

A website is a multi-page site with navigation, layout control, and blog support. Unlike single-page funnels built for one conversion action, websites give you a full web presence: home page, about, services, blog, and any other pages you need.

## What Your Agents Can Do

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Create a website** | Scaffold a new multi-page site with your campaign's theme |
| **Add pages** | Add new pages to the site (about, services, pricing, contact, etc.) |
| **Update pages** | Edit content, layout, and design of any page |
| **Set layout** | Configure the overall site layout and navigation structure |
| **Blog posts** | Create, edit, and manage a full blog within the website |

## Blog Posts

Every website can have a blog. Blog posts are first-class content with:

- **Title and slug** for clean URLs
- **Content** with rich formatting
- **Excerpt** for previews and cards
- **Cover image** for the post header
- **Author** attribution
- **Tags** for categorization
- **SEO settings** (meta title, description, etc.)
- **Status**: draft, published, or archived
- **Publish date** for scheduling

## How to Use

In Team chat, describe what you need:

- "Build a website for my consulting business with a home page, about, and services"
- "Create a company website with a blog section"
- "Add a pricing page to my website"
- "Write a blog post about our latest product launch"
- "Update the home page hero section with our new positioning"

Vibey builds the site, applies your campaign's [theme](/talents/themes), and handles the layout. If you've already built an [offer](/talents/offers), the website pulls copy from your positioning and buyer persona.

## Publishing

Websites publish to your [custom domain](/settings/connecting-your-domain), just like funnels. Set up your domain in **Workspace Settings → Domains** and any website can go live at your branded URL.

## Where Websites Live

Websites are artifacts inside a campaign. You can find them in:
- The **Artifacts** tab in Team chat preview panel (filtered separately from funnels)
- The **Campaign Dashboard → Deliverables** tab

<Callout type="tip">
  Websites and [funnels](/talents/funnels) are different tools for different jobs. Use a funnel when you need a focused page for one conversion action. Use a website when you need a full web presence with multiple pages and a blog.
</Callout>
