## What You Get

Your agents can generate images as part of any workflow. Whether you need ad creatives, social media graphics, hero images for landing pages, or thumbnails, your agents produce them inline alongside the rest of the work.

## Technology

Your agents use advanced image generation models powered by Gemini and Imagen to produce high-quality visuals. If a primary model hits a rate limit, the system automatically falls back to an alternative route so generation doesn't stall.

## How to Use

Ask your agent in Team chat or include it in a mission:

- "Generate an image of a modern workspace with warm lighting for our LinkedIn post"
- "Create 3 ad banner variations for our summer campaign"
- "Make a thumbnail for this YouTube video about productivity"
- "Design a hero image for our landing page"
- "Generate a product mockup on a clean white background"

## When Images Get Created Automatically

You don't always need to ask. When your agents build [funnels](/talents/funnels), create [ads](/talents/ads), or produce [social content](/talents/social-content), they can generate images as part of the process. If the task calls for a visual, the agent produces one.

## Where Images Live

Generated images are saved to your campaign's media library. You can find them in:
- The **Media** section of the preview panel
- The campaign's media view
- Attached to the deliverable or artifact that produced them

<Callout type="tip">
  Give your agent brand context. If you've uploaded brand guidelines or created a [theme](/talents/themes), generated images will better match your visual direction.
</Callout>
