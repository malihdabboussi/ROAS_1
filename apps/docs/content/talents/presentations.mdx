## What You Get

A presentation is a designed, slide-based document your agents create with layouts, fonts, colors, and images that match your brand. These are polished assets - not generic text dumps.

Use presentations for guides, checklists, workbooks, pitch decks, resource lists, or any structured document that needs to look professional.

## How to Create a Presentation

In Team chat, describe what you need:

- "Create a 10-step checklist for launching a podcast"
- "Build a workbook for setting quarterly business goals"
- "Make a resource guide with the top 20 tools for content creators"
- "Create a beginner's guide to email marketing"
- "Build a pitch deck for our investor meeting"

Vibey uses your [offer](/talents/offers), [buyer persona](/talents/avatars), and campaign/Space context to create content that's relevant and on-brand.

## Export Formats

Presentations can be exported in multiple formats:

| Format | Use case |
|---|---|
| **PDF** | Downloadable documents, lead magnets, email attachments |
| **PPTX** | Editable slide decks for PowerPoint, Google Slides, or Canva |
| **Text** | Plain text version for repurposing content |

Choose **Open in Canva** from the presentation export menu to create an editable Canva design. Vibey sends the deck as PPTX so Canva can preserve slide structure, text, shapes, and images instead of receiving one flattened screenshot per slide.

## How Presentations Connect to Funnels

The typical lead magnet flow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the presentation">
    Build a branded document in Team chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build a lead magnet funnel">
    Create a [landing page](/talents/funnels) with a form: "Enter your email to get the free guide."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Someone fills the form">
    They get the presentation. Their email lands in your [Contacts](/contacts/managing-your-contacts) with source tracking.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email sequence triggers">
    If you've set up a [nurture sequence](/talents/emails), it starts - following up with more value.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The presentation is the incentive. The funnel captures the lead. The email sequence nurtures them. All built and connected inside one campaign.

## Where Presentations Live

Presentations are saved as artifacts in your campaign. You can find them in:
- The **Artifacts** tab in Team chat preview panel
- The **Campaign Dashboard → Deliverables** tab
