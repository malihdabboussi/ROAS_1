## What You Get

An offer is the strategic foundation for everything else in a campaign. When Vibey builds an offer, it walks through six research-heavy steps and produces a workbook you can reference across funnels, ads, emails, and social content.

The finished offer includes:

- Product and market analysis
- A power offer statement with benefits, objections, and a call to action
- A deep buyer persona with psychological profiling
- An ideal customer profile with company-level targeting
- Competitive analysis with differentiating factors
- Your unique and differential mechanisms

## The 6-Step Process

Tell Vibey about your offer in Team chat. It runs each step sequentially, asking you questions and producing structured output at every stage.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Product & Market Analysis">
    What you sell and who you sell it to. Vibey analyzes your product details, target market, and the opportunity. This is the foundation that informs every step after it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Power Offer">
    The irresistible offer statement. Vibey produces your primary benefit, secondary and tertiary benefits, the vehicle (how you deliver), the most common objection, and a direct call to action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buyer Persona">
    A deep psychological profile of your ideal buyer. This goes beyond demographics into core problems, powerful emotions, biggest fears, past failed attempts, avoidance behaviors, perfect outcomes, transformation impact, success markers, blame targets, objections, internal voice, and content preferences.
  </Step>
  <Step title="ICP Analysis">
    The ideal customer profile at the company and organizational level. Includes company demographics, organizational details, pain points and goals, existing solutions they use, messaging triggers, buying triggers, psychographics, behavioral insights, cultural nuances, competitive landscape, and success stories.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Competitive Edge">
    What makes your offer unique in the market. Covers your company overview, included features, challenges solved, competitive advantages, differentiating factors, competitor analysis, success stories, and credibility proof.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unique Mechanisms">
    Your proprietary method - the thing only you do. Vibey identifies your unique mechanisms and differential mechanisms that make your offer impossible to replicate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens After

Your completed offer becomes a campaign artifact that everything else references:

- [Funnels](/talents/funnels) pull copy from the power offer statement and buyer persona
- [Ads](/talents/ads) target based on the ICP analysis and buyer triggers
- [Email sequences](/talents/emails) use the positioning and objection handling
- [Social content](/talents/social-content) hooks audiences using the persona's language and pain points
- The campaign stores the full workbook as project context so every agent working on this campaign can use it

<Callout type="tip">
  The offer is usually the first thing you should build for any new campaign. Funnels, ads, and emails all get better when they're built on a solid offer foundation.
</Callout>
