## The Limit Is What You Can Teach

If you can describe a workflow and give your agents examples of what great looks like, they can learn it, repeat it, and improve on it over time. Your agents are only as limited as your imagination and the skills you build for them.

## Business Operations and Beyond

Your agents aren't limited to marketing. People use Vibey to build custom dashboards, develop applications, import projects from GitHub, create widgets, automate business operations, run competitor research, generate financial reports, and manage client work. If an agent has the right skills, the right brain, and the right tools, it can do the work.

## Everything Stays Organized

Every artifact you create is automatically saved to the Space you are working in. You can find them in the relevant view (Funnels, Offers, Ads, etc.) inside that Space, in the Space's **Docs** view, or in the campaign's **Deliverables** tab.
