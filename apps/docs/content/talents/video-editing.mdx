## What You Get

Your Cloud Computer has ffmpeg installed - a professional video processing toolkit. Your agents can manipulate video files directly without external software.

## What Your Agents Can Do

| Operation | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Trim** | Cut a video to a specific start and end time |
| **Merge** | Combine multiple clips into one video |
| **Extract audio** | Pull the audio track from a video as MP3 or WAV |
| **Add audio** | Add a soundtrack, voiceover, or audio track to a video |
| **Resize** | Change dimensions (e.g., 1080x1920 for Stories, 1920x1080 for YouTube) |
| **Convert** | Switch between formats: MP4, WebM, GIF, MOV, MKV |
| **Extract frames** | Pull key frames from video for thumbnails or reference images |

## Supported Formats

| Type | Formats |
|---|---|
| **Video** | MP4, WebM, GIF, MOV, MKV |
| **Audio** | MP3, WAV, AAC, OGG |

## Video Analysis

Your agents can also analyze video content:

- **Transcribe speech** from any video using [Deepgram Nova-3](/talents/audio)
- **Extract key frames** for thumbnails or reference
- **Pull transcripts from URLs** - YouTube videos, podcasts, and recordings
- **Summarize video content** based on the transcription

## How to Use

Ask your agent in Team chat or send a mission:

- "Trim this video to the first 30 seconds"
- "Merge these 3 clips into one video"
- "Extract the audio from this recording"
- "Resize this video to vertical format for Instagram Reels"
- "Transcribe this YouTube video and give me the key points"
- "Extract a thumbnail frame from the 15-second mark"

<Callout type="tip">
  Drop a video file or URL directly into Team chat. Your agent can work with it immediately - trim, transcribe, convert, or analyze.
</Callout>
