## The One Sentence Version

Vibey is an AI workspace. Your humans and a team of AI agents work together inside it. They share one knowledge base (the Brain), one set of projects (Spaces), and one set of goals.

It is not a chatbot. It is not a single AI assistant. It is a place where work actually gets done.

## Why It Works

Most AI tools give you a chatbot in a tab. Vibey gives you a system with four parts that all work together:

<PlatformOrbital />

| Layer | What it is | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **The Brain** | Four living knowledge bases (User, Agent, Company, Customer) | Your agents already know your business before they start. No re-explaining every session. |
| **Your Team** | Named AI agents, each with a role, a brain, skills, and tools | Specialists, not generalists. A copywriter writes. An analyst analyzes. A CFO models. |
| **Spaces** | The workspace where work happens (views, docs, channels, flows) | Humans and agents on the same board. No parallel tools. No copy-paste between systems. |
| **Home** | Your daily landing page | One composer to start anything. Waiting items, recent conversations, your calendar, finished work. |

No single piece is the magic. The combination is.

## The Four Brains

Your agents are only as good as what they know. The Brain is how they know your business.

| Brain | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **User Brain** | Everything about you as an individual. How you think, how you communicate, what you know. |
| **Company Brain** | How your organization operates. Processes, decisions, institutional knowledge. |
| **Customer Brain** | Real signals from your real customers. What they say, need, object to, care about. Built from contacts and interactions, not invented personas. |
| **Agent Brain** | Role-specific expertise for one agent. The best of their field, focused on their specialty. |

A copywriter on your team reads from all four when writing copy. The output sounds like your business because it is built from your business.

Read more in [How the Brain Works](/brain/how-the-brain-works).

## Spaces: Where Work Happens

A Space is where a team operates. It contains:

| Surface | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Views** | 29 different ways to see the work. Tasks, contacts, media, funnels, emails, ads, analytics, presentations, social, and more. |
| **Docs** | One library for everything your team produces or references. Agent deliverables, meeting notes, strategy docs, uploaded files, Drive imports. |
| **Channels** | In-Space conversations. Humans tag agents. Agents respond, act, and produce output in the same thread. |
| **Flows** | Triggers and actions that run themselves. A Fathom meeting finishes, an agent suggests follow-up tasks. A form gets submitted, a contact gets created. |

Read more in [What a Space Is](/spaces/what-is-a-space).

## Who It Is For

Vibey is built for founders, operators, and teams who want AI to actually run parts of the business, not just answer questions.

You will get value if you have:

- A repeatable operation (content, ops, support, research, reporting)
- Real knowledge worth capturing (meetings, docs, customer conversations)
- A team that spends too much time switching between tools
- More work than the people you have
