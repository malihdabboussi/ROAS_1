## What Personal Space Is

Your **Personal Space** is a Space that belongs only to you. It is auto-created the first time you send a message from the [Home composer](/getting-started/the-home-page) with no destination picked.

Personal Space is private. Even inside an organization, no one else can see what is in your Personal Space.

## What Lives Inside

Anything you send through Home without picking another Space lands in your Personal Space:

- Conversations with Vibey
- Artifacts generated from those conversations
- Tasks you create for yourself
- Notes you write that are not meant for the org
- Files you upload for your own work

You can also add views, channels, and flows to your Personal Space, just like any other Space.

## Why It Exists

The Home composer is designed to be frictionless. You type, you send. No "pick a destination" pop-up every time.

Personal Space is what catches that input. It gives you a default place for personal work without forcing you to think about routing every message.

## Personal Space vs Org Spaces

| | Personal Space | Org Spaces |
|---|---|---|
| **Visibility** | Only you | Anyone with access in the org |
| **Created by** | Auto, the first time you send from Home | You or a teammate, explicitly |
| **Default destination** | Yes, for Home composer | No, unless you pick one |
| **Counts toward org billing** | Your personal account | Org account |
| **Survives org changes** | Yes, tied to your user account | Tied to the org |

If you leave an org, your Personal Space stays with you. Org Spaces stay with the org.

## Where to Find It

Personal Space shows up in your Spaces list with a personal icon. You can also click your avatar in the sidebar, which routes to a Personal scope view.

## Using Personal Space Intentionally

Some users use Personal Space as:

- A scratchpad for ideas before promoting them into an org Space
- A private notes layer for personal decisions and tracking
- A draft area for content before sharing
- A separate workstream from org work (side projects, personal writing)

Others mostly ignore it and route everything to org Spaces. Both are valid.

## Personal Space and the Brain

Personal Space is a workspace, not a brain. It does not have its own brain.

Conversations in Personal Space use your **User Brain** by default (since they are about you). If you tag a contact in a memory you save from Personal Space, that memory still flows to the **Customer Brain** at the org or personal level as configured.

## Sending From Home to a Specific Space

If you want a Home message to go somewhere other than Personal Space, click the **Send to** picker in the composer footer (next to Integrations). Pick the destination Space (Personal, or any org Space you have access to).

The composer remembers your last choice for the session.
