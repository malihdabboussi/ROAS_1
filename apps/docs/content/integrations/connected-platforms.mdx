Integrations give your agents real tools. Without integrations, agents can think and create. With integrations, they can act: posting content, publishing ads, syncing contacts, pulling analytics, managing files, and connecting to the platforms you already use.

## Content and Social

| Platform | What your agents can do |
|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Create and [publish posts](/talents/social-content) directly to your profile |
| **Instagram** | Publish posts, carousels, stories, and reels |
| **YouTube** | Analyze videos, extract content, pull comments, generate thumbnails |
| **Facebook** | Manage pages, track engagement |
| **Twitter/X** | Monitor engagement, research content |
| **TikTok** | Research content and performance |
| **Reddit** | Monitor subreddits, research content |
| **WordPress** | Publish and update posts, pages, media, categories, and tags |

<Callout type="tip">
  Want your agents to act on these platforms as you (posting, commenting, or researching private accounts you already follow)? Install [Vibey Mini](/vibey-mini/what-it-does) and share your signed-in sessions.
</Callout>

## How Integrations Connect to Agents

Integrations are assigned by **domain**. A marketing [agent](/team/meet-your-agents) gets marketing tools. A developer agent gets developer tools. This keeps agents focused - an agent with 5 relevant tools outperforms one with 50 random tools.

Higher-level agents (managers) can access more integrations than employees.

See [Setting Up Integrations](/integrations/setting-up-integrations) for how to connect everything.
