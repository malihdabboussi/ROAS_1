ROAS has its own MCP server at **`https://mcp.roas.io`**. Add it to your favorite AI tool — Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and others — and that tool can work with your ROAS workspace directly.

This means you can sit in Claude and ask it to search your Company Brain, save a note to your memories, create a campaign, or kick off a mission — and it all lands in ROAS, exactly as if you'd done it in the app.

<Callout type="info">
  Looking for the opposite — connecting outside tools *into* your ROAS agents? That's covered in [MCP Servers](/integrations/mcp-servers).
</Callout>

## What You Can Do

Once connected, your AI tool can:

| Area | What it can do |
|---|---|
| **The Brain** | Search your Personal, Company, Customer, and Agent Brains, and save new memories and knowledge |
| **Documents** | Read, search, and save documents in your Spaces, including downloadable Word docs |
| **Spaces & Tasks** | Browse your Spaces, views, items, and tasks |
| **Campaigns** | View your campaigns and create new ones |
| **Marketing** | View and create funnels, websites, pages, and email sequences |
| **Missions** | View your missions and send new ones to your team |
| **Agent Skills** | View your agents' skills and teach them new ones |

## What It Will Never Do

The ROAS MCP only exposes safe actions. It can read and create — it cannot destroy:

- ❌ No deleting anything
- ❌ No publishing pages or sending emails
- ❌ No billing or account changes

Every action also runs through the same permission checks as the ROAS app itself, so a connected tool can never do more than you can.

## Connecting

The address is the same everywhere: `https://mcp.roas.io`

<Tabs>
  <Tab label="Claude">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Connector Settings">
        In Claude (web or desktop), go to **Settings → Connectors**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add a Custom Connector">
        Click **Add custom connector**, name it **ROAS**, and paste `https://mcp.roas.io` as the URL.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign In to ROAS">
        Claude opens the ROAS approval screen. Sign in, choose your account, pick the permissions, and click **Allow**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start Talking">
        Try: *"Search my ROAS Company Brain for our pricing strategy."*
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="ChatGPT">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Connector Settings">
        In ChatGPT, go to **Settings → Connectors**. (Availability depends on your ChatGPT plan — connectors may be under **Apps & Connectors**.)
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add ROAS">
        Add a new connector with the URL `https://mcp.roas.io` and name it **ROAS**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign In to ROAS">
        ChatGPT opens the ROAS approval screen. Sign in, choose your account, pick the permissions, and click **Allow**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start Talking">
        Try: *"List my ROAS campaigns and summarize what's active."*
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Cursor">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open MCP Settings">
        In Cursor, go to **Settings → MCP** and click **Add new MCP server**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add ROAS">
        Name it **ROAS**, choose the **HTTP** type, and paste `https://mcp.roas.io` as the URL.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign In to ROAS">
        Cursor opens the ROAS approval screen in your browser. Sign in, choose your account, pick the permissions, and click **Allow**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start Talking">
        Try: *"Save this architecture decision to my ROAS user brain."*
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab label="Claude Code">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Add the Server">
        In your terminal, run: `claude mcp add --transport http roas https://mcp.roas.io`
      </Step>
      <Step title="Authenticate">
        Inside Claude Code, run `/mcp` and follow the sign-in flow for **roas**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start Talking">
        Try: *"Create a ROAS mission to review this week's funnel performance."*
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="tip">
  Any other app that supports remote MCP servers works too — just give it the address `https://mcp.roas.io` and sign in when asked.
</Callout>

## The Approval Screen

The first time a tool connects, ROAS shows you an approval screen before anything happens. This is where you stay in control:

1. **Choose the account** — your personal account or one of your organizations. The tool only sees that account's data.
2. **Set permissions per area** — for each area (Personal Brain, Campaigns, Marketing, and so on), choose **None**, **Read**, or **Write**. Want Claude to read your Brain but never change it? Set it to Read.
3. **Click Allow** — the tool is connected with exactly the access you granted, nothing more.

If you connect through an organization, your role there caps what you can grant — viewers can only grant read access, while owners and admins can grant everything.

## Built-In Guidance

The ROAS MCP doesn't just hand over tools — it teaches the connected AI how to use them well:

- Every connected tool receives instructions explaining what's safe, what each permission means, and how to call ROAS correctly.
- A built-in **docs search** tool lets the AI look up these very docs before acting, so it answers questions about ROAS accurately.
- A **describe action** tool lets the AI check exactly what an action needs before calling it, so fewer things go wrong.
