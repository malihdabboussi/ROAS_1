## Your Campaign Hub

Every campaign has its own dashboard. Click a campaign to see everything happening inside it. The dashboard has up to six tabs, and you can configure which ones are visible in Settings.

## The Tabs

### Missions

Your mission control for this campaign. Shows:

- **Mission status overview** with counts for each status: Inbox, Planning, Todo, In Progress, Review, Blocked, Done
- **Completions chart** showing finished missions over time (bar, area, line, or pie), filterable by time range (24h to 90 days)
- **Quick capture** to create new missions directly with title, priority, and attachments
- **Mission list or kanban board** with status filters, priority filters, search, and sort
- Click any mission to open its detail with subtasks, deliverables, activity timeline, and comments

### Team

The agents assigned to this campaign. Only assigned agents can execute [missions](/missions/what-are-missions) for this campaign.

- **Grid view** shows agent cards with avatar, name, role, and [theme](/talents/themes) badge
- **Details view** expands each card to show what the agent is currently working on and their recently completed missions
- **Manage your Team** opens the team management modal to add or remove agents

Different campaigns can have different teams. A copywriter with wellness-focused [skills](/team/agent-skills) on one campaign, and a copywriter with technical writing skills on another. The team assignment controls who does the work.

### Knowledge

Your campaign's brain and strategic directives. Split into three sections:

**Strategy and Directives**

The four fields that guide everything your agents do for this campaign:

| Field | What it answers |
|---|---|
| **Result** | What does success look like? |
| **Purpose** | Why does this campaign exist? |
| **Strategy** | How are we getting there? |
| **Off-Limits** | What will we NOT do? |

Also includes **Knowledge Search** to query the campaign's knowledge base and browse knowledge nodes.

**Brand Assets**

- **[Offers](/talents/offers)** connected to this campaign
- **[Avatars](/talents/avatars)** (customer profiles)
- **[Theme](/talents/themes)** (brand design: colors, fonts, logo, images, voice)

Click any asset to preview it, or open the theme editor to configure your brand identity.

**Agent Access**

A matrix showing which agents have access to which knowledge domains. Read-only view of how knowledge is distributed across your team.

### Deliverables

Everything your agents have produced for this campaign. When agents finish [mission](/missions/what-are-missions) subtasks, their outputs land here.

- **Search, sort, and toggle** between grid and list view
- **Preview any deliverable** directly (documents, images, videos, PDFs)
- **Turn deliverables into reusable context** by saving durable lessons to the right brain or converting repeatable patterns into agent skills
- **Download or copy** deliverables

### Finance

Revenue tracking and commerce management for this campaign. This tab is hidden by default and can be enabled in Settings.

- **Revenue overview** with metrics and time range filtering
- **Revenue chart** showing trends over time
- **Products** from Stripe with create/edit/detail management
- **Payment links** for direct sales
- **Coupons** for promotions and discounts

### Settings

Campaign configuration:

- **Tab visibility** - Choose which tabs appear in the campaign navigation. At least one must stay visible.
- **Danger zone** - Delete the campaign (requires typing the campaign name to confirm)

## Custom Views and Reporting

For richer, day-to-day workflows, create a [Space](/spaces/what-is-a-space) for this campaign and add the views you need: Lists, Boards, Calendars, Contacts, Media, Funnels, Emails, Ads, plus Reporting views (Campaign Overview, Funnel Analytics, Email Analytics, Ads Performance, Finance Overview).

Spaces give you 29 view types and let agents work in the same place humans do. The Campaign Dashboard tabs above remain useful for a quick top-level snapshot; the Space is where the team actually operates.

Read more in [Views](/spaces/views).
