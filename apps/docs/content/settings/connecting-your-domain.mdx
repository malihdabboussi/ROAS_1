## Why Connect a Domain

By default, your [funnels](/talents/funnels) and [websites](/talents/websites) are published to a Vibey subdomain like `yourname.vibeyfunnels.com`. Connecting your own domain means they show up at your branded URL, like `go.yourbrand.com` or `offers.yourbrand.com`.

This looks more professional, builds trust with your audience, and keeps everything on-brand.

## How to Connect a Domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workspace Settings">
    Navigate to **Workspace Settings → Domains**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your domain">
    Enter the domain or subdomain you want to use (e.g., `go.yourbrand.com`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your DNS">
    Vibey provides DNS records you need to add at your domain registrar. This is typically a CNAME record pointing your subdomain to Vibey's servers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for verification">
    DNS changes can take a few minutes to propagate. Vibey checks automatically and marks the domain as verified once it's ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish to your domain">
    When publishing a funnel or website, you can now choose your custom domain instead of the default Vibey subdomain.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Your URL Looks Like

| Setup | Your URL |
|---|---|
| **Default (no custom domain)** | `yourname.vibeyfunnels.com/sales-page` |
| **Custom domain connected** | `go.yourbrand.com/sales-page` |

## Multiple Domains

You can connect multiple domains if you need different URLs for different campaigns or brands. Each funnel or website can be published to whichever domain fits.

## Tips

- Use a **subdomain** like `go.` or `offers.` rather than your main domain. This keeps your primary website separate and avoids routing conflicts.
- **SSL certificates** are provisioned automatically - your pages always load over HTTPS.
- Connecting a custom domain doesn't break old URLs. Existing funnels published on the default subdomain continue to work.

<Callout type="tip">
  Set up your domain early. When your agents build funnels or websites, they can publish directly to your branded URL from the start.
</Callout>
