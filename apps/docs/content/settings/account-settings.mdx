---
title: "Your Profile"
description: "Manage your name, avatar, company, email, and password."
---

Open **Account Settings** from the sidebar and select **Profile**.

## What You Can Edit

| Field | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Full name** | How you appear across the platform |
| **Company name** | Your business name |
| **Avatar** | Your profile picture (JPG, PNG, or GIF, max 2MB) |

Your **email** (login address) and **User ID** are read-only and shown for reference.

## Changing Your Password

1. Enter your current password
2. Enter your new password
3. Confirm the new password
4. Save

Your password is updated immediately. You'll stay logged in on your current session.
