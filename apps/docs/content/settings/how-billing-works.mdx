Open **Account Settings → Billing** to manage your personal billing. For organization billing, see [Organization Billing](/organization/organization-billing).

## Credits

Vibey uses a credits-based system. Everything your agents do - [missions](/missions/what-are-missions), chat conversations, tool usage, media generation - consumes credits.

**200 credits = $1**

More complex tasks (Power models, multi-step missions, heavy tool use) consume more credits than simple ones.

## What Costs Credits

| Activity | Credit usage |
|---|---|
| **Chat conversations** | Based on [model tier](/team/meet-your-agents) and message length |
| **Mission execution** | Planning, subtask execution, review - each step uses credits |
| **Model tier** | Power uses more than Auto, Auto uses more than Economy |
| **Tool usage** | [Integrations](/integrations/connected-platforms), web research, [media generation](/talents/images) |
| **[Autopilot](/autopilot/how-autopilot-works)** | Autonomous work consumes credits like any other activity |

## Plans

Your billing page shows available plans:

- **Basic** - Starter credits and features
- **Pro** - Scaled credit allowances (configurable tiers from 8k to 110k credits)
- **Ultra** - Maximum allowance and features
- **Enterprise** - Custom pricing and at-cost credits

Switch between monthly and annual billing (annual saves 2 months). View, change, or cancel your plan from the **Package** tab.

## Auto-Recharge

Set up auto-recharge so you never run out mid-task:

1. Open **Account Settings → Billing**
2. Click **Auto Recharge**
3. Set your recharge amount and monthly cap

<Callout type="warning">
  If [Autopilot](/autopilot/how-autopilot-works) is running and credits hit zero, it pauses and your agents sit idle. Enable auto-recharge to keep operations running.
</Callout>

## Usage Tracking

Open **Account Settings → Usage** to see:

- **Credits remaining** - Base allowance, rollover, and purchased credits with progress bars
- **Recent activity** - Every credit debit with action type, feature, and timestamp
- **Monthly breakdown** - Where credits are going

## Invoices

The **Invoices** tab shows your past invoices. Click any invoice to view the full receipt. Access the Stripe billing portal to manage payment methods and download invoices.

## Subscription Management

From the **Package** tab:

- See your current plan, status, and billing cycle
- View Agent Brain add-ons and monthly total
- Change plans, manage your subscription, or cancel
- See your next renewal date and credit allowance
