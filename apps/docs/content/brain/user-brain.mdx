The User Brain is your personal knowledge base. It contains everything about you as an individual: your voice, your preferences, your experience, how you think and communicate. Every agent on your team can access it. This is the identity layer that all other brains build on.

## What Belongs Here

Knowledge that is about **you as a person**, not your organization:

- **Your communication style** how you write, how you talk, what tone you use
- **Your preferences** "we never use emojis in professional copy," "always lead with data"
- **Your personal context** your background, your origin story, what you have built before
- **Meeting recordings** rich context from real strategy calls and conversations
- **Your decisions** the choices you have made and why
- **Personal notes** ideas, frameworks you are developing, things you want to remember

If it is about how the **organization** works (processes, brand voice, SOPs across the company), it belongs in the [Company Brain](/brain/company-brain), not here.

If it is about a **customer** (real signals from real interactions), it belongs in the [Customer Brain](/brain/customer-brain).

## How to Add Knowledge

Go to the **Brain** page from the sidebar. Make sure your User Brain is selected in the scope picker. Click **Add Information** in the top right corner. You have three input modes plus an import menu:

### Text

Type or paste content directly. Great for quick notes, personal preferences, or frameworks. Add a title and hit save.

### Link

Paste a URL and Vibey extracts the useful content from the page. Works with blog posts, articles, documentation, and any public web page.

### Voice Recording

Click the microphone to record a voice memo. Vibey transcribes it and saves the content as a memory. Useful for capturing ideas on the go.

### Import

Click **Import** to access additional sources:

| Source | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Upload from local** | Drag and drop PDFs, Word docs, spreadsheets, text files, audio, or video. Supported: `.txt`, `.md`, `.csv`, `.json`, `.xml`, `.yaml`, `.pdf`, `.docx`, `.png`, `.jpg`, `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.mp4`, `.mov` |
| **Google Drive** | Browse and import files from your connected Google Drive |
| **Dropbox** | Browse and import files from your connected Dropbox |
| **Fathom** | Select specific meeting recordings to import (requires Fathom connected) |
| **Fireflies** | Select specific transcripts to import (requires Fireflies connected) |
| **Media Library** | Import from your Space media assets |

## Connecting Fathom and Fireflies

Meeting recordings are some of the richest knowledge sources. Connect your meeting tools in **Workspace Settings → Brain**:

### Fathom

1. Connect your Fathom account from **Workspace Settings → Brain**
2. Toggle **auto-ingest** on. New meetings are automatically added to your Brain without lifting a finger
3. Or import specific meetings manually from the **Add Information → Import → Fathom** menu

### Fireflies

1. Connect your Fireflies account from **Workspace Settings → Brain**
2. Run a **manual sync** to pull in transcripts
3. Or import specific transcripts from the **Add Information → Import → Fireflies** menu

## Managing Your User Brain

### Search

Search across all your memories by topic, keyword, or meaning from the Brain page search bar.

### Delete

- **Delete a single memory** removes that piece of knowledge and its connections to other memories
- **Delete a source** removes the source and cascades to all memories extracted from it. Delete a PDF, and every piece of knowledge from that PDF is removed.

### Copy and Move

Transfer memories to a [Company Brain](/brain/company-brain), [Customer Brain](/brain/customer-brain), or [Agent Brain](/brain/agent-brain):

- **Copy** keeps the original in your User Brain
- **Move** removes it from your User Brain

### Talk to Atlas

[Atlas](/brain/atlas-the-brain-scholar) is your Brain Scholar. Talk to Atlas from the Brain page to ask questions about your knowledge, save new memories conversationally, search across everything you have stored, and identify gaps.

### Crystallization

Over time, your Brain accumulates many individual memories. Crystallization is the process of organizing and connecting them: finding patterns, grouping related knowledge, and strengthening the graph. Atlas can trigger crystallization to keep your Brain well-structured.

## Brain Health

The Brain settings page in **Workspace Settings → Brain** shows your connection status for Fathom and Fireflies, auto-ingest settings, and overall brain health.

<Callout type="info">
  Everything in the User Brain is accessible to all agents on your team (unless you toggle off User Brain access for specific agents in their [configuration](/team/configuring-agents)). If something should only be known by one agent, put it in their [Agent Brain](/brain/agent-brain) instead.
</Callout>

## The Difference It Makes

**Without User Brain:** "Write a LinkedIn post about our new product."
→ Generic post with placeholder language that could be about anyone's product.

**With User Brain loaded:** Same request.
→ Post that uses your vocabulary, matches how you actually talk, and reflects the personal context that makes it sound like you wrote it.
