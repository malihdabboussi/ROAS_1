The Agent Brain is personal to one agent. While the [User Brain](/brain/user-brain) is about you, the [Company Brain](/brain/company-brain) is about your organization, and the [Customer Brain](/brain/customer-brain) is about your customers, the **Agent Brain** is about being the best at one specific role. Project-specific context lives in campaigns and Spaces.

The idea: **give your agent the best of the best in their field.** If it is a copywriter, feed it the top copywriters' work. If it is an analyst, the best research frameworks. The agent learns to perform at that level.

## What to Upload

Think about what would make this agent world-class at their [role](/team/meet-your-agents):

| Agent Role | Brain Content |
|---|---|
| **Copywriter** | Top copywriters' portfolios, swipe files, proven headline formulas, ad breakdowns |
| **Content Producer** | Expert video tutorials, production frameworks, scripting templates |
| **Social Media** | Viral post breakdowns, platform-specific best practices, engagement case studies |
| **Brand Manager** | Award-winning brand guidelines, design trend reports, brand strategy frameworks |
| **Analyst** | Research methodologies, analysis frameworks, report templates, benchmarks |
| **Developer** | Architecture patterns, coding standards, documentation templates |
| **Customer Support** | Response templates, tone guidelines, escalation frameworks |
| **Finance / CFO** | Financial modeling frameworks, reporting standards, KPI definitions |
| **Sales** | Discovery question frameworks, objection handling scripts, top performer call transcripts |

## How to Train an Agent's Brain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the agent">
    Go to the Team page and select the agent you want to train.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open their Brain">
    On the Brain page, pick that agent in the scope picker. Or open the agent's profile and navigate to their Brain section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload expert content">
    Drop in YouTube videos, PDFs, articles, frameworks. Anything that represents excellence in their field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it process">
    The Brain automatically extracts key principles, techniques, and patterns. Wait for processing to complete.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review what was extracted">
    Check the knowledge nodes. You will see extracted concepts, frameworks, and techniques. Delete anything that is not relevant or accurate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing Agent Brain Content

### Search

Search the agent's brain to find specific knowledge by topic or keyword.

### Delete

- **Delete individual memories** that are not relevant
- **Delete a source** to remove all knowledge extracted from one upload

### Copy and Move

- **Copy** knowledge from any other brain into an agent's brain
- **Move** knowledge between brains when it belongs somewhere else

### Knowledge Graph

Each agent's brain has its own graph visualization showing sources and the entries extracted from them. See how knowledge connects and identify gaps.

## Brains That Do Not Exist Yet

Not every agent on your team will have a brain on day one. The Brain scope picker shows two lists:

- **Agents with a brain** these have been activated and are ready to train
- **Agents without a brain yet** these need their brain activated first

Activate an agent's brain from their profile to start training them.

## The Payoff

An agent with a trained Brain produces fundamentally different output:

**Without Agent Brain:** Generic content that follows basic patterns anyone could produce.

**With Agent Brain trained on top performers:** Content that follows proven structures, uses advanced techniques from the best in the field, and demonstrates real expertise.

The time investment is minimal: uploading a video or document takes seconds. The quality difference compounds with every task.
