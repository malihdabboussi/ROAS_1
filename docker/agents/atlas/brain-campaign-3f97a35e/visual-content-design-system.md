## Context

Sefy's visual content style is codified and intentional. Every design decision reflects a philosophy: raw intelligence made legible. These rules apply to all content — carousels, reels, BTS videos — and must be followed consistently.

## Core insight

The aesthetic is builder authenticity meeting technical authority. No stock photos, no corporate polish, no performance. Real screenshots, real terminals, real products.

## The story

**Visual Philosophy:** Content sits at the intersection of builder authenticity and technical authority. The system should feel like what it is: a CTO who builds real products, shows his actual screen, and explains complex things clearly. 'Raw intelligence made legible' is the guiding phrase.

**Carousel Hook Slide Rules (Slide 1):**
The hook slide has one job: stop the scroll and create an open loop. Rules are strict:
- Maximum 7 words in the hook statement
- No more than 2 elements on the slide (headline + optional texture)
- Use a product screenshot or terminal window as background texture when possible
- No decorative frames, no heavy gradients, no stock photography
- The text must create tension or curiosity without resolving it

**CTA Slide Strategy (Final Slide):**
Converts attention into action. Design: subtle violet gradient glow (radial, low opacity) in the top-left corner signals energy and transition. CTA lines matched to post type:
- Product build BTS: 'We’re building this live. Follow to see what ships next.'
- Educational/framework: 'Save this. You’ll use it next week.'
- Proof/results: 'DM me [keyword] and I’ll send you the full breakdown.'
- Platform launch: 'Join [X] builders already inside. Link in bio.'

**Video Text Overlay Protocol (Reels/BTS):**
- Font: Inter Bold, all caps for emphasis words, mixed case for sentences
- Color: #F8F8FF (white) primary, #7B5CF5 (Electric Violet) for key terms or product names
- Style: clean, minimal animation
- Subtitles: auto-generated, reviewed for accuracy, formatted in short bursts (3-5 words per line)
- No text backgrounds, no speech bubbles, no lower-thirds bars
- Placement: avoid the bottom 20% of frame (subtitle zone) and the top 10% (safe zone)

## Key takeaways

- Hook slide: 7 words max, 2 elements max, use real product screenshots as texture
- CTA: violet gradient glow, match copy to post type — never generic
- Video text: Inter Bold, #F8F8FF primary, #7B5CF5 for key terms, no backgrounds
- Never use stock photos or decorative frames
- The entire aesthetic is 'CTO showing his actual work' — authenticity over polish, always
- Electric Violet (#7B5CF5) is the brand accent color for emphasis across all formats
