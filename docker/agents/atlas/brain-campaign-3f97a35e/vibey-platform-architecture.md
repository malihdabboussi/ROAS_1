## Context

Vibey is a two-sided AI marketing platform built around a three-brain knowledge architecture. Understanding the structure is essential for explaining, selling, and operating within it.

## Core insight

The platform isn't just a toolset — it's an AI organization operating system where the brain stores context, agents execute work, and campaigns are the project layer that coordinates goals across channels.

## The story

Vibey is split into two sides:

**Create Side** — marketing execution tools: funnels (generate and one-click publish), lead magnets, email sequences, ads (launch directly to Meta), content generation (carousels, reels, blog posts), image and video generation. Canvas-based workflow where you connect a funnel to a sequence with drag-and-drop logic.

**Manage Side** — the AI organization layer: agent hiring via HireAI, the campaign brain, mission system, and team management. This is where the multi-agent hierarchy lives.

The knowledge layer underneath both sides is the **Three-Brain Architecture**:

1. **User Brain** — personal knowledge extracted from every conversation and Zoom call. Atlas captures 'neural snapshots' with 16 data points (facts, decisions, insights, stories, frameworks, preferences) and connects them to related memories. Grows automatically over time.

2. **Agent/Specialist Brain** — domain expertise specific to each agent role. A copywriter agent holds brand voice, tone, and copy examples. A designer holds visual guidelines. Knowledge is role-scoped — workers don't see strategy, managers don't get lost in brand colors.

3. **Campaign Brain** — business-specific knowledge: offers, avatars, funnel data, market research, performance results. Shared across agents working on that campaign.

The **Orchestration Framework**: Brain = memory. Agents = workers. Campaigns = project layer that executes goals using the brain's context.

## Key takeaways

- Always frame Vibey as two things: a marketing execution platform AND an AI organization
- The three-brain system is the core differentiator — context compounds over time
- Agents need role-appropriate knowledge: managers get strategy/purpose/results, workers get offers/avatars/brand guidelines
- The create side is the deliverable engine; the manage side is the intelligence layer
- Campaigns are how goals get executed — they bridge the brain to the output
