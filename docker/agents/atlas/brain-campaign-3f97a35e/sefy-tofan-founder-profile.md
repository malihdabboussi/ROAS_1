## Context

Sefy Tofan is the founder of Vibey, based near Ayia Napa, Cyprus. Understanding his background, daily habits, and philosophy is essential for authentic communication, sales framing, and content that reflects his actual experience.

## Core insight

Sefy built Vibey to solve his own problem: he hit the limits of existing tools (OpenClaw) and needed a proper AI organization platform with security, scalability, and persistent brand context.

## The story

**Origin:** Sefy built Vibey by taking the multi-agent communication concept from OpenClaw and turning it into a full AI organization platform. His original multi-agent setup on OpenClaw hit hard limitations: security issues, couldn't create persistent brand context, couldn't scale properly. He needed something purpose-built.

**Daily CEO Workflow:** Every morning after dropping kids at kindergarten and his wife at work, Sefy asks his AI CEO agent: 'Give me the report. What have we accomplished in the last 24 hours? What is blocked? What needs my attention? What do I need to move forward?' He gets a morning briefing before anything else. This is the exact workflow he demos to prospects as a proof point that the system actually works in his own life.

**Sales Style:** Sefy runs impromptu demos casually — not scripted. He explains complex AI architecture concepts in accessible, concrete terms. He uses live examples from his own experience (the CEO agent evolution, the proposal skill) rather than hypotheticals. His framing is founder-to-founder, not vendor-to-customer.

**Background:** Not primarily a developer background — built Vibey by understanding what an AI-powered organization should feel like from an operator's perspective. He went from running multi-agent setups on OpenClaw to building the infrastructure others can use.

**Visual Design Philosophy (for content):** Sefy's visual content aesthetic sits at the intersection of builder authenticity and technical authority. The system should feel like what it is: a CTO who builds real products, shows his actual screen, and explains complex things clearly. No stock photos, no corporate polish. Raw intelligence made legible.
