## Core Philosophy

Offer is the highest-leverage skill. Upstream design choices (offer structure, price anchoring, ascension path) determine downstream results before execution begins. A structurally sound offer with mediocre copy outperforms a mediocre offer with exceptional copy.

**Dylan's non-negotiable at intake:** If the offer hasn't been validated (live sales, organic proof, existing results), he pushes back on running paid traffic. ICP clarity is non-negotiable. Would rather lose the deal than inherit a structurally broken engagement.

## Three-Part Trust Funnel

Dylan's foundational architecture for coaching and course businesses:
1. **Free community** (Skool or equivalent) — entry point, builds audience
2. **Low-ticket offer** ($27-$697) — qualifies buyers, creates ascending intent
3. **Webinar or VSL to high-ticket** — converts pre-warmed audience at scale

Each tier writes back to the same brain/audience. Each tier makes the next tier more effective.

## Low-Ticket Ascension Funnel

**Three-tier architecture (used with Christian Osgood / Multifamily Strategy):**
1. Ultra-low-ticket entry at $3.05 (priced to match a Starbucks drink — memorable branding, near-zero psychological friction)
2. Mid-ticket course bundle with surprise over-delivery bonus + free CRM/tracker tool (creates ecosystem habits and dependency)
3. VSL-to-book-call routing to $10K mentorship

**The principle:** Once someone has paid even $3 and is actively using your tools, they're far easier to convert on high-ticket. The CRM tool is not a perk — it's an ecosystem lock-in mechanism.

**Low-ticket qualifier rule:** A $27-$50 entry offer serves as a buyer qualifier, not just a revenue driver. People who pay a small amount demonstrate seriousness. Roughly 70% of low-ticket buyers will schedule a call on the confirmation page. Closers who get these leads are working pre-qualified prospects who have already demonstrated willingness to invest.

**Accepting 20% return on low-ticket ad spend is correct** because the real monetization happens downstream through the VSL and high-ticket funnels.

## Multi-Funnel Architecture

Don't extract maximum revenue from any single touchpoint. Run three parallel funnels:
1. **Low-ticket book funnel** ($3.05 entry) — top-of-funnel volume, brand awareness, list building
2. **Evergreen VSL funnel** — always-on revenue engine, profitable continuously
3. **Periodic webinar funnel** — highest converting but event-based; run every 2 weeks

Funnels feed each other: webinar non-buyers get retargeted into VSL; low-ticket buyers ascend through courses to VSL book-call.

## Post-Purchase Disguised Qualification

After a low-ticket purchase, frame the 'create your account' flow as deep qualification. Buyers are in a committed, optimistic mindset right after purchasing — they'll answer detailed questions (income, journey stage, goals) when framed as 'personalizing their experience.' Survey responses then route to: custom content recommendations, different email sequences, or direct sales call booking. This produces far richer data than a cold survey and routes people to the right conversion path automatically.

## Webinar Offer Pricing Architecture

For high-ticket webinars, the multi-tier close:
- Price-anchor at $10K mentorship (make it feel real)
- Drop to $697 course with hard scarcity (limited spots, link turns off live)
- Upsell on confirmation page to $5K tier (two payments of ~$1,997)
- Soft-CTA non-buyers to VSL book-call flow after urgency window closes

Benchmark: Spanish-market comparable — $10K ad spend, 3,700 registrations, 600 attendees, $29K in sales at ~$1K offer, 5% close rate, 25% upsell take rate.

## Guarantee Architecture

Guarantees are sales tools first, protection mechanisms second. A well-structured guarantee reduces friction at the top of funnel and enables leading with it in ads and VSLs.

**Activity-based guarantee structure (used with Multifamily Strategy):**
- Full refund available at month 6 only if the student can prove they completed specific monthly activity requirements (number of offers made, owner calls, broker calls, 75% Zoom attendance)
- A tracking portal monitors compliance
- This structure de-risks for serious buyers while protecting against refund abuse
- Creates accountability that improves student outcomes, generating more testimonials

**Proposed guarantee (Ben Jordan, outcome pending):** '2x revenue in 90 days or I pay you $3K' — bold guarantee designed to improve VSL conversion in the trades coaching space.

## Offer Validation Before Scale

Core belief: never scale ad spend on an unvalidated offer. The Lo Silver lesson is the reference point. Proof of concept must come before paid traffic investment. Validation means: live sales, organic conversions, or real client results at the stated price point.

## Pricing Philosophy

- **Value-based, not cost-based:** priced against the ROI the client will achieve, not the cost to deliver
- **Never discount:** if the price is too high, the scope changes, not the price
- **Don't ask for $10K on a webinar:** price anchoring is the bridge between perceived value and commitment
- **People don't spend $7K on personal health, but they do on business investments:** reframing from 'expense' to 'investment with ROI' changes who buys and how easily they justify it

## CTAs and Positioning

Generic CTAs ('strategy call,' 'audit,' 'growth audit') have become repellent — immediately recognized as sales calls. Fix: create outcome-specific CTA names tied to the client's exact situation ('visibility review,' 'speak with [Name] directly,' 'the Digital Handshake session').
