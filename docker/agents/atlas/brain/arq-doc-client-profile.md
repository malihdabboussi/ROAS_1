## What Arq Doc Is

Arq Doc is an emerging premium hardware brand building a portable laptop workstation for remote workers, entrepreneurs, freelancers, and gamers. The product combines a stable laptop surface with cable management, USB/USB-C connectivity, mouse support, drink holders, and phone props — positioning it as a portable workstation platform, not a basic lap desk.

## Launch Context

- **Stage:** Pre-launch, preparing for global rollout
- **Target markets:** USA, Australia, Europe
- **Stakes:** Client has expressed this is their one major shot at going global. Launch must stick.
- **Available assets:** Framer landing page invitation, Google Drive folder with marketing assets

## Positioning Strategy

**Target audience:** Remote professionals and gamers who want a premium hybrid work-and-play setup — integrated cable-managed connectivity, design-driven ergonomics, and a clean home environment.

**Key positioning pillars:**
- Integrated cable management and connectivity (not just a lap surface)
- Design-driven ergonomics for serious remote work
- Bridges work and play aesthetics without compromise

**SEO / brand naming challenge:** Arq Doc faces name-collision risk with unrelated Arqdoc/Arq Doc entities online. Limited public digital footprint at launch. Category descriptors are important to establish early so search results cluster correctly around the product category (portable workstation / laptop workstation) rather than noise.
