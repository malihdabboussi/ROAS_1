## ROAS.co Meta Ads Protocol

**Placements (always manual):**
- REMOVE: Messenger, search results, explore pages, multi-advertiser ads, apps & sites
- KEEP: feeds, profiles, stories, reels (99% of attention)

**Ad Set Structure:**
- Narrow + Broad + Advantage+ (if budget allows only 2, do Narrow + Broad)
- Never start with Advantage+ alone
- Test variables one at a time: copy vs creative vs audience
- Kill underperformer at 2x target CPL with no conversions

**Campaign Launch Handoff includes:**
- Client name, campaign objective, daily budget, avatar description link, creatives folder, ad copy link, launch date
- Publish campaign TURNED OFF, copy link, post in ads channel, tag AM lead

## KPI Benchmarks
- CTR: over 1%, ideally 1.5%+, 2%+ is ideal
- CPM: under $50, ideally ~$30
- Frequency: flag if above 2.0 (audience saturation)
- Cost per application: $20-30 for survey/lead gen funnels
- Target webinar CPL: $10-15
- YouTube Shorts: 19% view rate, $0.08 average CPV is good

## Diagnosis Framework
- Compare CPC vs cost per link click vs cost per landing page view to isolate where the funnel breaks
- Low CPC but high cost per link click = ad copy problem (not creative/image)
- Quality ranking below average + engagement ranking above average in Meta confirms copy problem
- High CPM = audience saturation or wrong audience; cut budget immediately
- When frequency is above 2.0, rotate creative or expand audience

## Meta Andromeda (Algorithm Sequencing)

Meta Andromeda automatically sequences ads for individual users. A 'high cost' ad may be warming the audience before a lower-cost converting ad closes the sale. Rule: never turn off an ad based on cost alone without understanding its role in the sequence. Feed diverse creative formats and let Andromeda find the right sequence. Killing warming ads prematurely breaks the conversion chain.

## Video Ads as Targeting Mechanisms

Meta's algorithm distributes video ads based on what is being said, not just audience targeting parameters. A hook that says 'Hey, if you're a tennis coach...' reaches tennis coaches through content relevance. Video hooks function as both creative AND targeting mechanism. Format: 'I'm looking for 3-5 [specific ICP] who want to [specific outcome].' Change the ICP callout to create multiple segment-specific variants from one shoot.
