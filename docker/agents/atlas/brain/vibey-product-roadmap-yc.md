## Y Combinator Application (May 2026)

Vibey applied to Y Combinator. The application process was strategically valuable beyond the outcome — forcing the team to answer 'What do you understand that competitors don't?'

**Key insight crystallized through YC prep:** The Brain product (AI memory retrieval and structured context across Slack, Zoom, Gmail, call recordings) is a standalone breakout product, not just a feature of a marketing platform. The Instagram reel confirming 'AI memory retrieval is the biggest unsolved AI problem' appeared organically the same day the AG1 founder (through Sefy's wife's connection) encouraged the YC application — treated as a signal confirmation.

**Refined YC pitch positioning:**
- Problem: companies have knowledge scattered across tools (Slack, Gmail, Zoom, Docs, Meetings)
- Solution: Vibey connects those tools and turns scattered knowledge into structured memory, giving humans and agents the ability to co-work in one place
- Three pillars: structured brain for companies + agents that deeply understand the business + spaces where humans and agents work together

**Working YC application description (May 2026):** 'Vibey gives AI agents a real brain so they can actually understand your business instead of guessing. Right now, every AI tool starts from zero every time. It doesn't know your customers, your decisions, your processes, or why you do things the way you do. Vibey fixes that with a structured memory system. It connects your existing tools and converts information into memories: facts, decisions, insights, frameworks, and techniques. Each memory is tagged with context and emotion. Over time, Atlas (the brain scholar agent) maps these memories into perspectives, then a full worldview. The result is an AI that actually knows your business and gets smarter the more you use it.' Current traction at time of application: $20K MRR.

## Near-Term Product Priorities (Sefy-Led, May 2026)

1. **Mobile optimization** — immediate priority, blocking some use cases
2. **Slack integration via Composio** — integration backbone enabling team use outside the platform
3. **New onboarding flow** — asks business questions, then connects communication platforms (WhatsApp, Slack, Telegram) during setup so users experience Vibey outside the app from day one
4. **Unified dashboards** — aggregate analytics from YouTube, Instagram, Google into one view; consistently requested by enterprise CMOs
5. **Organic social content and multi-platform posting** — users want to repurpose YouTube videos into LinkedIn posts; Israeli partner building YouTube channels specifically requested this workflow
6. **Newsletter capabilities**

**Communication preference in onboarding (live feature):** After naming their CEO agent, users can connect Telegram and set it as their default communication channel. CEO agent then proactively reaches out with updates. UX insight: this choice must happen during onboarding, not buried in settings.

## Voice-First Roadmap (Product Vision)

The 'always-on Vibey' concept is a core product direction with multiple layers:

**Voice interaction (near-term):**
- Open any agent, toggle voice using Gemini Flash (near-zero latency)
- Natural conversation where the agent executes actions, creates artifacts, responds in real time
- Not a dictation tool — a thinking-out-loud interface where the agent actually does the work

**Chrome extension (mid-term):**
- Gives agents screen awareness
- Use case: browse Instagram, verbally ask Vibe to recreate a carousel the user is looking at
- The extension bridges the gap between the browser as the knowledge environment and Vibey as the execution environment

**Multi-agent voice calls (longer-term):**
- Zoom-style call where multiple agents each have their own voice and collaborate in real time
- Becomes the 'morning meeting' format: brief your AI team, they add insights, you set the day
- This is the 'always-on Vibey' expression at full deployment

## Long-Term Hardware Vision

Dylan's ultimate vision extends beyond software: a wearable device (analogous to a Whoop band) that acts as a life companion — always listening, ready to execute tasks on voice command (send emails, manage workflows, capture decisions as memories).

The hardware layer is explicitly in the pitch deck product roadmap (Phase 3 / Vision layer). It is not near-term — it is the narrative endpoint that gives investors a reason to believe in the $2B Series B vision.
