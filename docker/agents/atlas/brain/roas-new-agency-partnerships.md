## The Subcontract/Fulfillment Partner Model

This partnership structure (ROAS as white-label fulfillment behind another agency) is distinct from:
- Direct client retainers (ROAS is visible, contracts directly)
- Revenue-share partnerships (1DS/AOS model — no retainer, profit split)

The subcontract model makes sense when:
- The other party controls the client relationship and sales system
- ROAS's contribution is execution-only (creative, media buying, funnels)
- The retainer is lower ($4K/month vs. $5K+ direct) but volume can compensate

**Network validation:** The Brian Rand deal was possible because of significant client network overlap in the event and coaching space. Shared history reduces trust-building friction and allows faster deal closure.
