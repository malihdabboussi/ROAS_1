## What This Is

Impact Elite Coaching is a ROAS client in the mortgage/real estate coaching space. Campaign strategy documented June 2026. Focused on live event marketing ('The Lab') targeting high-producing mortgage professionals.

## Target Avatar — The Stuck Operator

- Mortgage lenders producing $20M-$100M/year ($250K-$1M personal income)
- Have hit a growth ceiling — not beginners, established producers
- Fear the complacency of mid-level success more than outright failure
- Require structural operational changes, not motivational coaching
- Digital presence and positioning lag far behind their actual real-world reputation and credibility
- This is a frequent pattern for established offline businesses transitioning online

**Key insight:** For high-performing professionals who have achieved moderate success, the psychological threat of becoming comfortably stagnant is often a more powerful marketing hook and motivator than fear of failure.

## Competitive Positioning in Mortgage/Real Estate Coaching

Two distinct positioning lanes in this market:

1. **Modern digital marketing/prospecting** — sells motivation and personal brand (Shawn Kaplan's CCU plays here)
2. **Scaling as an operator** — sells operational change and measurable results (where Impact Elite positions)

Impact Elite's positioning: operational transformation and immediate execution ('a Monday-ready plan'), delivered by active top producers — not retired practitioners or theory-only coaches.

**Key differentiator:** Speakers/instructors are still actively producing at the top of the industry. Credibility comes from current practice, not past results.

## The Lab Event

**What it is:** Live in-person event targeting the Stuck Operator avatar

**Ticket pricing:**
- General Admission: $2,497
- VIP: $3,997

**Ad budget:** $20,000 total, split equally — $10,000 for a promotional webinar + $10,000 for direct event page ads

**Target:** 100 ticket sales

**VIP value bridge:** Grant immediate access to digital training assets (e.g., Sales Academy) up to 90 days prior to the physical event. Reduces the gap between purchase and the event date, increasing perceived immediate value.

## Launch Strategy — Forward Event Launch Model

1. Use a free webinar as the primary ticket launch vehicle
2. Offer launch-only pricing during the webinar
3. Run simultaneous retargeting ads displaying progressive 'sold %' metrics to drive FOMO
4. This creates a natural urgency loop without requiring manufactured scarcity claims

This model is consistent with the **Live Event Sellout Launch Sequence** documented in the Webinar Funnel Playbook, validated here in a mortgage industry context.

## Connection to ROAS Framework

The Stuck Operator avatar parallels a broader positioning insight: established businesses often have a credibility gap between their offline reputation and online presence. Closing this gap with content and paid traffic creates outsized returns because the underlying authority already exists — it just needs distribution.
