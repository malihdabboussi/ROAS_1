## The Core Principle

Build the offer first, then the content. The most common webinar mistake is building content slides and leaving the close section to the last minute. Everything in the webinar must tie back to the single transformation the offer delivers.

**Webinar build order:**
1. Define the offer and what's being sold
2. Outline the deliverables
3. Build scarcity, urgency, and risk reversal elements
4. Determine the one core perspective shift for the content section
5. Build the content that leads naturally into that offer

## Webinar Offer Structure

For high-ticket offers ($6K-$16K+), the structure has four components: **Product, Price, Scarcity, and Risk Reversal** (plus bonuses). Key sequencing: never reveal the price before explaining what's in the product.

**Price anchoring sequence (four methods in descending order):**
1. What you personally achieved with this system (highest anchor)
2. What you personally invested in time and money to get here
3. What results other clients achieved
4. What competitors charge for similar offerings

Spend 3x as long on bonuses as the core offer — bonuses are what push fence-sitters to buy. People perceive they're paying the discounted price for just the core offer but getting all the bonuses free.

## Multi-Tier Pricing Strategy

Instead of pitching $10K directly on a 90-minute webinar:

1. **Price-anchor** to $10K mentorship, make it feel real and desirable
2. **Drop to $697 course offer** with hard scarcity (limited spots, link turns off live)
3. **Confirmation page upsell** to $5K mentorship tier (two payments of ~$1,997)
4. **Soft CTA** after course closes: drop a link to the VSL funnel for non-buyers ('I'm not going to ask you for $10K on a webinar, but if you know you need this, here's a link')

This captures revenue at three price points from a single webinar: $697, ~$4K upsell, and $10K via VSL. Reference benchmark: Spanish-market webinar at $10K ad spend, 600 attendees, 5% close rate, 25% upsell take rate = $29K.

## Show Rate Optimization

**Proven notification sequence for 80-100+ attendance:**
- Email day before
- Email morning of
- Email one hour before
- Email 15 minutes before
- Text message at exact start time (key change that boosted attendance in Shawn Kaplan's CCU data)

**Database reactivation:** Hit previous registrants with a simple email/text sequence within the 48-hour window before the event. Keep warm list, cold list, and paid ad leads siloed to get clean data on conversion rates per source.

## Funnel Architecture (High-Ticket Coaching)

**Standard funnel steps:**
1. Registration page with form
2. Confirmation page with video (set expectations, build anticipation)
3. Live webinar: story, vision-cast, business model, investment range, CTA to book or buy
4. Booking page with optional qualifying questions
5. Post-webinar follow-up for non-buyers (email urgency, then book-a-call option)

**Attendance benchmark:** About 1/3 of registrants show up live. From 60 attendees, expect ~15 call bookings and 3 closes. Ads: ~$5/lead for broad biz-opp audiences.

**Cooling problem:** A webinar-only funnel creates a 9-day cycle from initial interest to sales conversation (register day 1, wait up to 7 days for webinar, book a call 1-2 days after). Hot leads driven by emotional pain can cool off completely. Solution: run an evergreen VSL call-booking funnel alongside periodic webinars. Leads who don't book on the VSL get retargeted to the next webinar. Leads who don't convert on the webinar get routed to the VSL.

## Backend Sequencing (Post-Webinar)

A key rule: whatever revenue you generate during the live webinar, target at minimum the same amount from backend follow-up with the same attendees. If the live webinar generates $10K, the backend should generate at least another $10K.

**Post-webinar sequence:**
1. Two days of urgency emails (special price expiring)
2. After urgency window closes, introduce book-a-call option ('maybe you still have questions')
3. Sales rep gets webinar attendees as hot leads first, registered non-attendees as secondary tier

**Retargeting (the Stalker Method):** After someone books a call, retarget them with 10 short videos addressing common objections between booking and the call. This keeps prospects warm, builds trust, and improves show rates and close rates on booked appointments.

## Production Quality

Elevating from 'guy on Zoom' to a produced experience dramatically increases conversion. A dedicated event space with multiple camera setups, professional production team, camera cuts, and surprise guest appearances creates an Apple-keynote-style feel. The production quality signals legitimacy and makes the audience feel part of something bigger than a typical online pitch.

## Live Event Sellout Launch Sequence

For live in-person events with ticket sales:
1. Build free webinar launch event funnel targeting 10,000 opt-ins
2. Seed organic content 2 weeks out (not earlier — people don't plan far ahead)
3. Activate community affiliates with referral leaderboard and exclusive prizes
4. Run paid ads segmented by awareness level (unaware, problem aware, solution aware)
5. On launch day: webinar starts at 10am, tickets go live at 10:15am
6. Launch retargeting ads for the event page the moment tickets go live
7. Pre-record urgency videos at sold-out percentages: '80% sold out, get your ticket now'
8. Send text sequence throughout the day with sold-out milestones
9. Update ad creatives hourly with sold-out percentages
10. After sellout, pivot to secondary high-ticket offer while audience is still hot
