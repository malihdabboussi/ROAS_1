## What This Is

A revenue-share partnership between Dylan Vanas / ROAS and 1DS (John Hyland) / AOS (Art of Selling, co-owned by John Hyland and Sam Parham). First documented May 2026.

## The Parties

- **Dylan Vanas / ROAS.co:** owns all marketing execution — traffic, funnels, ads, webinar builds, copy, tech/automation, sales infrastructure
- **1DS (John Hyland):** brand execution, content vision, brand oversight
- **AOS — Art of Selling (John Hyland + Sam Parham):** owns the IP, course content, community fulfillment. AOS is trademarked. 1DS's stated non-negotiable: 'The only thing we are protective of with anything and with anybody is that AOS remains IP.' Everything else is negotiable.

## Two-Phase Structure

**Phase 1 — 90-day sprint:**
- Two test campaigns to prove the business model
- 1DS funds the financial risk: $15K total ad spend ($7.5K per campaign)
- Dylan/ROAS invests: services (funnels, ads, creative, sales expertise)
- Neither side takes a retainer in Phase 1
- Success threshold: each campaign breaks 3X ROAS on cash collected; if either falls under 3X, the team pauses and restructures before pushing more spend
- Goal for Phase 1: everyone at minimum breaks even and generates operating capital

**Phase 2 — Long-term profit share:**
- Rolls in after Phase 1 data confirms the model works
- Structure: profit-share on net revenue (revenue minus shared operating costs)
- Specific profit split to be agreed upon after Phase 1 results come in
- No retainer structure in Phase 2 either

## Open Architecture Question (As of May 2026)

The single biggest unresolved item blocking Phase 1 launch: **offer architecture.** The choice between a low-ticket digital course, high-ticket coaching program, or community subscription dictates everything downstream:
- Sales team required (yes for coaching, maybe no for low-ticket VSL)
- Fulfillment commitment (10 community members = full year of community management)
- Platform choice
- Entire economic model
