## The Standard Nick Post Blueprint

Every piece of Nick's content follows a consistent structure:

1. **Hook (0-3 seconds or first sentence):** Stat, challenge, or personal admission that signals this is about the reader's actual problem. Examples: "95% of agents fail. Here's why." / "In 2016, I was just a broke agent."
2. **Context (1-2 sentences):** Why this matters right now.
3. **Payload:** The insight, framework, or story. Tight and specific. Real numbers.
4. **CTA:** One clear action. Never two.

This blueprint applies across formats: Reels, TikTok, captions, and email subjects.

## Objection Handling Style

Nick leads with specificity on price objections. Example: reframing a $500K life policy as $45/month, "that is one dinner out." The pattern is: acknowledge the objection, reframe with a concrete comparison, close fast.
