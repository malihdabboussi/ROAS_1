- Each two-person unit can handle 15-20 clients

## Team Leadership Transition Pattern

Dylan uses a consistent leadership promotion model documented across multiple team members:
1. Identify the team member doing strong execution work with leadership potential
2. Backfill their execution tasks with a junior hire
3. Gradually shift responsibilities from execution to leadership while they train their replacement
4. Give the transitioning person increasing ownership over team coordination

**Applied cases:**
- Jill: graphic designer promoted to Creative Director / QC lead
- Georgette: social media manager transitioning to Video/Social Team Lead (part-time SM assistant approved May 2026 to backfill execution; full-time hire and full elevation if new SM client signs)
- Payal Chandak: campaign manager to Media Buyer Lead (effective April 2026)

This prevents talented execution people from stagnating. The transition period carries risk — the transitioning person may be nervous about solo leadership responsibilities (Georgette cited this). Staged handoffs with explicit coaching sessions are the fix.

## Campaign Packet System

Every new client receives 4 deliverables:
1. **Strategy Brief** — ICP/avatar, offer positioning, funnel type, market research
2. **Funnel Build** — opt-in, VSL/webinar reg, thank-you, upsell (all built in GoHighLevel by Raffae after Figma mockup stage)
3. **Email Sequence** — nurture/confirmation copy matched to campaign type
4. **Ad Copy** — 2-3 Facebook concepts (static + video scripts)

**Slack Review Flow:** Strategy brief → Slack → team approves → build → approve creative → deploy. Campaign launch handoff includes: client name, campaign objective, daily budget, avatar description link, creatives folder, ad copy link, launch date. Publish campaign TURNED OFF, copy link, post in ads channel, tag AM lead.

## Delegation & Oversight Rules

- Delegation without oversight creates invisible failures: media buyers trained to spend full budget will keep spending on failing ads without a flag system
- Fix: require weekly performance reports from every media buyer
- Check every ad account at least once per week
- Rule: if a campaign is outside KPI for 48 hours, flag immediately and reduce spend
- Trust but verify, especially with offshore team members who may lack full context
- When a campaign is performing well, do not change the budget or budget strategy — it negatively affects performance (validated by James, May 2026)
