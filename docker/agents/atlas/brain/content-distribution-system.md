## The 7 Content Pillars

Dylan's content system runs on 7 recurring themes, distributed across platforms rather than siloed by channel:

1. **Entrepreneur's Real Story / Real Talk** — transformation arcs, failure-to-success, raw moments
2. **Founder's Mindset** — success patterns, entrepreneur identity, reframes
3. **Offer & Sales** — funnels, pricing, one-to-many mechanisms
4. **Success Patterns** — frameworks extracted from others' behavior
5. **Personal Brand** — what a real personal brand is and why it compounds
6. **Agency / Business Builder** — client work, team building, behind-the-business
7. **Build in Public** — transparent documentation of what's being built and why

Pillars are staggered across platforms and days to prevent feed repetition. No single pillar dominates any given week.

## Platform Calibration

**Instagram (@dylan_vanas)**
- Hook in line 1, 100-250 words, story + framework + CTA
- Reels primary format; carousels for frameworks
- Optimal timing: 6:00 PM CT (peak evening engagement)
- 6 posts per 4-week cycle

**LinkedIn (/in/dylanvanas)**
- Long-form text posts, business outcomes prominent, slightly elevated register
- Structure: hook + context + insight/list + CTA
- Optimal timing: 8:00 AM CT (professional morning consumption)
- 6 posts per 4-week cycle

**X (@dylanvanas)**
- Sharpest and most concise, one idea per post, contrarian one-liners
- Threads for frameworks (Agency Model, Success Patterns, Build in Public)
- No filler, zero hedging
- Optimal timing: 9:00 AM CT, daily or near-daily
- 8 posts per 4-week cycle (5 standalone + 3 threads of 5 tweets)
- Highest volume platform — primary discovery and thought leadership channel

**Rule:** No two platforms post simultaneously. Pillars are staggered to avoid feed repetition.

## 4-Week Posting Architecture

Total: 20 posts per cycle (6 LinkedIn + 6 Instagram + 8 X)

**Week 1:** Mon LinkedIn (Entrepreneur Story), Tue X (Founder Mindset), Wed Instagram (Entrepreneur Story), Thu X (Offer/Sales), Fri LinkedIn (Success Patterns), Sat X (Personal Brand)

**Week 2:** Mon Instagram (Success Patterns), Tue LinkedIn (Agency/Business Builder), Wed X (Success Patterns), Thu Instagram (Build in Public), Fri X (Entrepreneur Story), Sat LinkedIn (Personal Brand)

**Week 3:** Mon X Thread (Agency Model), Tue Instagram (Founder's Mindset), Wed LinkedIn (Offer and Sales), Thu X Thread (Success Patterns), Fri Instagram (Personal Brand), Sat LinkedIn (Mindset/Identity)

**Week 4:** Mon Instagram (Real Talk), Tue X Thread (Build in Public), Wed LinkedIn (Flex), Fri X (Offer/Sales)

## Hook Formats That Work

- 'Most [type] do [mistake]. Here is what actually works.'
- 'In [year], I [story moment] and it taught me [one thing].'
- Opening mid-story for maximum pull
- Contrarian claim as opener for X one-liners

Hook sensitivity: a single word change in the opening hook determines whether a piece receives thousands or millions of impressions. Format selection is an upstream design decision that determines reach before execution begins.

## Personal Brand Philosophy

Personal brand is the most valuable asset you own that no one can take from you. Platform algorithms change and ad costs rise, but a personal brand compounds. Most people treat Instagram like a diary; successful people treat it like a media company.

Podcast content (AlchemistCEO, formerly Mindful Leaders) is also a distribution vector: LLMs increasingly source information from podcast transcripts. Strategy: keep 85% organic/natural, layer 15% strategic content through titles and repeated messaging across episodes. Consistent podcast messaging reinforces the narrative being pushed through PR articles and website content.

## Growth Sequencing for Zero-Budget Builders

1. Mine existing relationships first (historical client list, existing contacts to email list)
2. LinkedIn organic: personal + company page content, 3x/week, engage with others
3. Run monthly educational webinars using existing contacts and LinkedIn network
4. Only after proving the organic concept (2-3 webinars generating deals) invest in paid ads to scale what's working

This de-risks early paid ad investment and creates proof of concept before scaling.
