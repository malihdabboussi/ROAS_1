The Vibey Platform and Investor Relations pages cover business context. This page consolidates the specific pricing model, credit economics, enterprise tier details, and the Adli/Viralish distribution deal that were documented in memory clusters but not captured in library pages.

## Enterprise Pricing Structure

**Primary enterprise tier: $9,500/month for a 3-month engagement**
- One-time setup session
- Weekly 1-hour calls
- On-demand support
- Custom platform feature development
- At-cost usage (API/credit costs passed through, not marked up)

**Rationale for at-cost credits:** Eliminates the 'double payment' objection ($9.5K retainer + credits on top). Prevents clients from wanting to bring their own API keys and restructure the deal. Actual at-cost price can improve over time as Vibey negotiates bulk provider rates — hidden margin improvement without changing the client-facing narrative.

**Post-3-month enterprise access:** minimum $3,000/month base + $300 per user per month.

**Design partner framing (Sefy's model):** Early enterprise clients are framed as 'design partners' — they pay $9.5K/month, get custom feature development, can request features directly, and their feedback shapes the product roadmap. Distinction: no traditional retainer because it's not service-based. Clients pay for platform access plus credits/usage, not for ongoing human services.

Three pricing tiers built for investor pitch deck and VC conversations. As of May 2026, these are sales anchors, not yet built into the platform:

## Credit Economics

**Retail pricing benchmark:** 10,000 credits = ~$50 retail. At cost: ~$8-9.

**Real use case benchmark:** A complete campaign for a vitamin company (scanning 23 product pages of 1,500+ words each, competitive analysis, legal analysis, creating an ad campaign with 3 ad sets and 2 ads each including image generation and copy) cost 2,500 credits in power mode (best models). That campaign = ~$12.50 retail or ~$8-9 at cost.

**Enterprise example:** Adli received 500,000 credits for her initial $8-10K engagement fee.

## Dual Business Model (OpenWonderful.ai Inspiration)

Dual model inspired by Israeli company OpenWonderful.ai, which raised $5M using this approach:

1. **Tier-based SaaS** for B2C clients: free, $20, $40, $200 tiers (scalable, self-serve)
2. **Service as Product** for enterprise: $50K-$100K/year, dedicated team helps brands integrate systems, build custom skills, onboard onto platform

Discovery was organic: enterprise clients like Adli wanted hands-on setup rather than self-service, revealing that the enterprise buyer wants a different engagement model than the self-serve buyer.

**Who Adli (Adley Kingsman) is:**

**The JV deal structure:**
- Adli bundles a 1-year Vibey platform license into her course at $1,000 per signup
- Vibey charges Adli ~$100-$1,000/month per user she brings in
- Joint launch to the Viralish community planned for June 2026
- This is the most important near-term growth event for Vibey's user count

**White-label declined:** Adli originally wanted to white-label Vibey. Dylan declined:
- Too early-stage: 'You can't build a brand if nobody knows Vibey exists'
- Adli chose not to white-label because she didn't want to handle support

**Template implication:** The Adli/Viralish JV is now the documented template for Vibey's creator distribution model. Any creator with large audience + content community + desire to teach tools = potential JV candidate.
