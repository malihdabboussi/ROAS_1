## QC Standards for AI Output

AI-generated content must pass a 'does this sound like a real person' test before client delivery. Specific standards enforced:

1. **Script authenticity:** AI-generated ad scripts flagged as 'really AI' in tone are rejected. Scripts must match how the client actually talks, not generic motivational copy.
2. **Voice cloning:** Use ElevenLabs V3 for higher realism. Use per-line tone prompting to control delivery style.
3. **Audio/visual sync:** Fix sync bugs where the wrong character's voice plays over another character's face before delivery.
4. **Ad compliance:** Meta's AI now transcribes video content and flags non-compliant spoken words even when written copy passes review. A list of words and phrases to avoid in video audio was distributed to the team.
5. **CTA format:** Final card CTA should say 'Click the link below' (not display a specific URL) to drive ad clicks.
6. **Disclaimer placement:** Disclaimer text must run continuously at the bottom of the video, positioned below captions, for the full video duration — not just at the end.
7. **Creative direction:** Prioritize unique, distinctive visual concepts over simple talking-head formats whenever the client has a product.
