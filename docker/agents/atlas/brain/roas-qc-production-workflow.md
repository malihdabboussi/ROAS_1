## Why This Page Exists

As of May 2026, ROAS implemented a new structured QC and creative production workflow, led by newly hired Operations Lead Ana Carolina. This page documents the system architecture, role responsibilities, escalation rules, and ClickUp workflow structure.

Prior state: the founder was the catch-all for quality. Every QC issue eventually reached Dylan. The new system is designed to systematize ownership away from the founder.

## The Three-Tier QC System

**Tier 1 — Builder Self-Check** (quick, pre-submission)
- URL functional
- CTA correct
- Mobile device test passed
- All copy read through once

**Tier 2 — Ops Lead Core Check** (10-item checklist, ~10 minutes)
- Covers: copy, visual, mobile, pre-publish items
- Uses conditional models based on project type:
  - Pages for paid traffic: specific compliance model activates
  - Regulated niches: compliance model activates
- Separate checklists for landing pages vs. ad creatives vs. organic content
- Each checklist embedded as a ClickUp task template by service type and player role

**Tier 3 — Account Manager Final Check** (minimal, 2-minute pre-send)
- Last verification before client delivery

**What the Ops Lead does NOT escalate:** technical bugs, typos, broken mobile layouts, anything that can be technically verified. These are resolved without founder involvement.

## The Six Escalation Criteria (to Founder)

The ops lead only escalates to Dylan when one of these six conditions is met:
1. Client-first deliverable issues
2. Legal or compliance claims
3. Deviation from brief
4. Media spend exceeding threshold
5. Tiebreaker situations the ops lead cannot resolve
6. Receive-that-to-work items

If the founder is needed for normal technical calls, the ops lead role is not functioning as intended.

## ClickUp Workflow Status System

Task statuses mirror the creative production flow:

`Planning` > `Progress` > `Review` > `Operations for Review` > `Founder` (only if 6 criteria met) > `Client Revision` > `Rejected` > `Ready to Send` > `Sent to Client` > `Approved by Client`

**The client portal** is reserved only for deliverables that are ready and have been corrected. Not for drafts or in-progress reviews.

## Creative Production Workflow (Full Sequence)

1. **AM Brief Intake:** complete brief with all assets required — tasks cannot advance without inputs
2. **Builder Planning:** self-checklist completed
3. **Ops Lead QC Review:** core 10-item checklist
4. **Founder Escalation:** only when 1 of 6 criteria met
5. **AM Final Check:** before client send
6. **Client Review and Approval**

Key principle: 'luck by design' — tasks cannot advance without required inputs at each stage.

## Weekly Meeting Cadence (Established May 2026)

Three calls per week, totaling 1.5-2 hours maximum:

1. **Monday:** Reviewer/Builder technical standup — ClickUp blockers, one QC pattern from last week, scorecard review
2. **Tuesday:** Account Manager sync — operational review
3. **Friday:** Founder sync — leadership L10 format

A ClickUp database tracks all meetings with dates and structured agendas.

**Pre-existing cadence:**
- Monday 11 AM Pacific all-hands: Dylan leads Builders breakout, Nate leads Account Managers breakout
- Friday Victory Lap: share wins, connect as team
- Dylan does weekly 1-on-1s with team leaders

## Known Adoption Challenges

- Account Managers bypass the portal by assigning work via Slack (convenient but breaks QC tracking). Builders are more process-compliant after 3-4 weeks of training.
- Unit leaders bypassing the portal to assign video tasks directly in ClickUp disrupts workload distribution. Enforcement: Georgette screenshots bypass assignments and sends to Dylan.
- Portal campaigns feature is underutilized due to team confusion; needs training/tutorials.

## Communication Protocol (Ops Lead to Stakeholders)

**To Account Managers:** Open casually, frame QC as a system job from sentence one. Position ops lead as an ally, not a watchdog. If AMs see a watchdog, they resist; if they see an ally, the flow works.

**To the Founder:** Status, Action, Risk, Timeline format. Concise. Take ownership with 'won't bring you this again.'

**Dylan's escalation preferences:** Default async Slack. If no response, tag in Slack. If still unresponsive, WhatsApp. Dylan does not prefer voice notes.
