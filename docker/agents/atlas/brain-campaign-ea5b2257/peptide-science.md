Peptide therapeutics represent a distinct class of medicine: they don't replace the body's biology, they amplify it. This page synthesizes the clinical evidence, mechanism science, safety data, and compound-level intelligence across the peptide domain, drawn from 14+ PubMed studies (2025-2026), Huberman Lab episodes, and the InbarMD physician practice model.

**Core insight:** Peptides function through signal amplification, not replacement. GH secretagogues stimulate the pituitary's natural release rather than injecting synthetic hormone. BPC-157 upregulates growth factor receptors rather than supplying exogenous growth factors. This "work with the body" mechanism is what makes them safer than traditional hormone replacement and what makes physician oversight the differentiator, not the restriction.

## How Peptides Work: The Signal Amplification Model

The fundamental principle separating peptides from traditional pharmaceuticals is that they amplify existing biological signals rather than overriding them. Where exogenous hormone replacement can suppress natural production (creating dependency), peptides like GH secretagogues stimulate the pituitary gland's own release, preserving receptor sensitivity and natural pulsatile patterns.

BPC-157 exemplifies this: it upregulates growth factor receptors rather than supplying growth factors directly. The EGR-1 transcription factor activation it triggers leads to 2-3x VEGF upregulation for angiogenesis and modulates nitric oxide actions (neutralizing cytotoxic effects while maintaining protective functions). This is why BPC-157 requires active tissue injury for maximum effect: it amplifies the body's repair response, so there must be a repair signal to amplify.

This context specificity extends across the class. GH secretagogues require administration timed with sleep architecture (30-60 minutes before sleep) to exploit the body's natural growth hormone pulse. Incorrect timing can negate clinical effects entirely. Efficacy is determined equally by timing and dosage.

## Compound-Level Clinical Evidence

### Repair Peptides (Tier 1)

**BPC-157** is the lead compound with the strongest safety profile. The first-in-human IV administration study (2025) recorded no serious adverse events up to 20mg IV dose, confirming decades of animal safety data translating to humans. At 10x therapeutic dose, no tumor promotion or organ pathology was observed.

Key clinical data points:
- Oral formulation confirmed for gut epithelial repair: ZO-1 and occludin upregulation (tight junction proteins), with significant IL-6 and TNF-alpha reduction
- Per-oral therapy reversed quadriceps detachment and restored muscle function in animal models (2025), confirming oral bioavailability for complex tissue repair
- Neuroprotection: preserves dopaminergic neurons in TBI models, reduces 4-HNE oxidative stress markers
- BPC-157 targets angiogenesis and has neuroprotective potential in Parkinson's and Alzheimer's models

**BPC-157 + TB-500 combination** outperforms either compound alone. The 2:1 ratio (BPC-157 to TB-500) produces synergistic (not merely additive) tissue repair. EGR-1 transcription factor activation is the primary molecular driver.

**TB-500 / Thymosin Beta-4** provides systemic repair and is paired with BPC-157 in the "healing stack." Tier 1 risk profile.

### GH Secretagogues (Tier 2)

**CJC-1295 without DAC** (2025 kinetic study): Preserves natural pulsatile GH pattern with no HPTA suppression confirmed at 12 weeks. This is the key safety differentiator versus synthetic HGH; receptor sensitivity is preserved.

**Ipamorelin** (2025 crossover RCT): Confirmed as the cleanest GHRP. Only 12% cortisol elevation versus 38% for GHRP-2. Prolactin not elevated. Selective GH release only.

**Sermorelin** (2026 RCT, adults 45+, 6-month): Sleep quality +31% (subjective and PSG-measured), body fat -4.2%, lean mass +2.8%, zero adverse events. FDA-approved compound with the strongest regulatory standing of any GH secretagogue.

**MK-677** (24-month follow-up): Lean mass +1.4kg sustained, bone mineral density increased at lumbar spine. Monitoring essential for insulin-resistant populations as fasting glucose can increase 8-12 mg/dL. GH pulsatility amplitude increased 60-70% over baseline. Oral, FDA-restricted.

**Tesamorelin** (non-HIV metabolic syndrome study): Visceral adipose tissue reduced 15.2% over 26 weeks, fasting insulin sensitivity improved, LDL-C unchanged. FDA-approved for HIV lipodystrophy, off-label metabolic use supported.

GH secretagogues carry 5+ years of documented compounded use data in the US, with a 5-year follow-up study (2026, n=218) showing zero malignancy signal, no insulin resistance in metabolically healthy subjects, bone density maintained or improved, and IGF-1 within physiological range under supervised cycling.

### Longevity Peptides

**Epithalon** (10-year follow-up, 2025): Telomere length 15% longer versus control, telomerase activity elevated throughout, circadian cortisol pattern normalized (morning high, evening low restored), pineal gland function supported. Tier 1 risk profile, though long-term human data remains limited.

**NAD+ combined with BPC-157** produces additive mitochondrial biogenesis and inflammation reduction. The combination suggests a synergistic framework for longevity and recovery treatments.

### Cognitive & Neuropeptides (Tier 3)

**Semax** (2025 RCT): Working memory improved under cognitive load, cortisol stress response reduced 28%, BDNF increased 40%. Dose: intranasal 300mcg. Strong evidence for executive function support. Phase II clinical data confirms improved working memory and reduced anxiety in healthy adults.

**Selank** (2025 RCT): GAD-7 anxiety score reduced 42% versus 18% placebo, HPA axis normalized, no dependence profile at 8 weeks. Provides anxiolytic efficacy comparable to benzodiazepines without dependence risk, functioning through GABA-A modulation and BDNF upregulation. Ideal for high-performers with stress-driven anxiety versus clinical anxiety.

### Female Health

**PT-141 / Bremelanotide** (2025 real-world registry, n=312 women): 71% HSDD response rate with 12-month durability confirmed. Post-menopausal response comparable to premenopausal, which is a significant finding. FDA-approved for HSDD in women. Requires blood pressure monitoring.

**CJC-1295/Ipamorelin in perimenopause** (RCT 2025): Deep sleep +24%, lean mass preserved, hot flashes -31%, IGF-1 normalized in 82% of subjects. FSH, LH, and estradiol were not disrupted. This is the first major RCT confirming GH secretagogues are safe during hormonal transition.

### Emerging Research

GH Secretagogues (e.g. JMV2894) are showing therapeutic potential in preclinical models for Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy, signaling expansion of peptide use into neuromuscular medicine.

## The Risk Framework

**Three-tier risk system:**
- Tier 1 (Lowest risk): BPC-157, TB-500, Epithalon
- Tier 2 (Moderate risk, physician required): CJC-1295, Ipamorelin, Sermorelin, GHRPs, Tesamorelin
- Tier 3 (Experimental): PT-141, MK-677, Kisspeptin, Semax, Selank, Cerebrolysin, Pinealon

This is a research maturity rating, not a protocol ranking. The InbarMD physician selects based on patient labs, history, and goals.

**Three risk categories** (per Huberman): (a) Tumor promotion, especially with GH secretagogues in patients with cancer history; (b) Endocrine disruption from axis suppression with long-term unsupervised use; (c) Contamination from unregulated sources. Physician-supervised protocols address all three: bloodwork flags category (a), protocol design prevents (b), pharmaceutical sourcing eliminates (c).

**Cycling is non-negotiable** for axis-affecting compounds: 8 weeks on, 4 weeks off minimum for most GH secretagogues.

## The Sourcing Crisis

A 2025 independent purity analysis found 59% of commercially available peptide products were either underdosed (>20% below stated quantity) or contained detectable contamination. LPS (lipopolysaccharide) contamination from bacterial endotoxins in non-pharmaceutical-grade peptides causes inflammatory responses and is identified as the #1 sourcing risk.

The clinical standard requires:
- Licensed 503A/503B compounding pharmacy (FDA-registered facility)
- Batch-specific Certificate of Analysis (COA)
- Pharmaceutical-grade excipients and carriers
- Proper cold chain from pharmacy to patient

Peptide purity directly determines efficacy and safety. Impure or improperly folded peptides can produce inflammatory, allergic, or null responses.
