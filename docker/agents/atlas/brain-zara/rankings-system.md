# Tennis Europe Junior Tour Rankings

## How Rankings Work
The Tennis Europe Junior Tour uses a 52-week rolling ranking system. Points are accumulated over the past 52 weeks (1 full year), and older results drop off automatically.

## What Counts Toward Rankings
- Best 6 singles results
- Best 2 doubles results
- Best 2 team event results
- Lower-grade results can count multiple times in certain cases

## Who Has Rankings
- 14 & Under: Yes
- 16 & Under: Yes
- 18 & Under: Yes
- 12 & Under: NO individual rankings. All players in 12&U receive Organiser Acceptances (OA) for draws.

## Tournament Categories and Points
- Category 1: Highest points available (top-tier events)
- Category 2: Mid-tier points
- Category 3: Lower points (all 12&U events are Cat 3)

## Where to View Rankings
- Viewable on the Tennis Europe website at tenniseurope.org
- Rankings update on a rolling basis as new results come in

## Key Regulation References
- Tour Structure: tenniseurope.org/page/38980/Tennis-Europe-Junior-Tour-Structure
- Full Rules: tenniseurope.org/page/16456/Rules-and-Regulations

## Common Player Questions
- Q: Why did my ranking drop? A: Rankings are 52-week rolling, so old results fall off over time.
- Q: My 12&Under child has no ranking - is that normal? A: Yes, 12&Under players do not have individual rankings by design.
