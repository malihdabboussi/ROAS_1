# Tennis Europe Coach Education

Tennis Europe runs a comprehensive coach education structure across its 49 active member nations, in partnership with the ITF.

## Main Programs

### Coach Education
- Covers coaching conferences, courses, and workshops
- Key documents: Comparison chart of European coach education systems, Rio Maior Convention, European framework for coaching qualifications
- Contact available for all 49 member nations
- Page: tenniseurope.org/page/15946/Coach-Education

### Coach Education Systems in Europe
- Overview of coach education structures across Europe
- Page: tenniseurope.org/page/36485/Coach-Education-Systems-in-Europe

### Coaching Conferences
- Historical events: Play and Stay (2009), 10 & Under Tennis (2011), annual Coach Education Conferences (2013-2019)
- Page: tenniseurope.org/page/15891/Coaching-Conferences

## Development Programs

### Tennis Europe Academy
- Launched in 2018 for 14&Under elite players from 'C' nations
- Players nominated by their national federations
- Partners: Czech Tennis Association and TC Prostejov
- Head coaches: David Kotyza and Jaroslav Navratil
- Page: tenniseurope.org/page/36133/Tennis-Europe-Academy

### Junior Development Initiatives
- Touring teams for 14/16/18 & Under age groups
- Top coaches travel with teams
- Includes 14 & Under Development Championships
- Page: tenniseurope.org/page/33335/Junior-Development-Initiatives

### Tennis Europe Junior School
- Free educational program for 14/16 & Under players at Junior Tour events
- Topics: coaching, tennis ecosystem, social media
- Available in multiple languages: English, Spanish, French, Russian
- Page: tenniseurope.org/page/36514/Tennis-Europe-Junior-School

### Development Projects (ITF-supported)
- Coaches Courses
- Worldwide Coaches Conference
- Tennis Europe Coaches Conference
- Page: tenniseurope.org/page/35311/Development-Projects
