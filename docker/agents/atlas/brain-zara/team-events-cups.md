# Tennis Europe Team Events

Tennis Europe runs two major team competition series where nations compete against each other: Summer Cups and Winter Cups.

## Summer Cups
- Europe's longest-running junior team championships
- Nations field teams of junior players
- Players compete for their countries
- Age groups: Boys/Girls 12&Under, 14&Under, 16&Under, 18&Under
- Top two nations from each group qualify to the next round
- Page: tenniseurope.org/page/16418/Tennis-Europe-Summer-Cups

## Winter Cups
- Indoor team competition equivalent to Summer Cups
- Same national team format
- Runs during winter months

## How Nations Qualify
- Nations are nominated by their national federations
- Teams are selected and nominated by each federation before deadlines
- Groups are formed regionally, with top performers advancing

## Important Notes for Support
- Team events do NOT count toward individual tournament limits (e.g. the 12&Under 10-tournament annual cap excludes team events)
- Players participating in team events still need valid IPIN and Tennis Europe accounts
- Nomination deadlines for Summer Cups extend well in advance
