# Tennis Europe - Who They Are and What They Do

Tennis Europe is the regional governing body for tennis across Europe, operating under the ITF (International Tennis Federation). Founded on May 31, 1975 in Rome by 17 European national federations, it has grown into the world's largest regional ITF association with 50 member nations.

Headquartered in Basel, Switzerland, Tennis Europe administers over 1,200 tennis events annually spanning junior tours, team championships, development programs, and coach education initiatives.

## Core Mission
Tennis Europe exists to develop, promote, and govern tennis across Europe at all levels, from grassroots junior participation through to elite performance pathways.

## Key Pillars
- Junior Tour: Year-round competitive circuit for players aged 12, 14, 16, and 18 & Under
- Team Events: Summer Cups and Winter Cups where nations compete
- Development Programs: ITF-backed initiatives covering coaches, training centers, junior pathways, and facilities
- Coach Education: Structured qualifications, conferences, and workshops across 49 member nations
- Tennis Europe Academy: Elite training program for top 14&Under players from developing nations

## Key Facts
- Founded: May 31, 1975, Rome, Italy
- Headquarters: Basel, Switzerland
- Member Nations: 50 (Russia and Belarus suspended since 2022)
- Events per year: 1,200+
- Website: tenniseurope.org
- Junior contact: juniors@tenniseurope.org
