# Most Common Tennis Europe Support Questions

This page covers the top questions Zara should be prepared to answer confidently.

## Registration and Account Questions

**Q: How do I get an IPIN?**
A: Go to itftennis.com, create an account, verify your identity, and pay US$60 annual fee. This is valid for 12 months.

**Q: Do I need an IPIN for 12&Under events?**
A: Yes. IPIN is required for all age groups. However, players only in 12/14/16&Under Tennis Europe events do NOT need to complete ITF online courses.

**Q: How do I create a Tennis Europe account?**
A: Go to tenniseurope.org/member/createaccount.aspx and register. You will use this account to enter tournaments.

**Q: My IPIN expired - what do I do?**
A: Renew via itftennis.com > My Profile > Circuits > Renew Membership. Pay the annual fee again.

## Tournament Entry Questions

**Q: How do I enter a tournament?**
A: Preferred method is online at tenniseurope.org. You can also email juniors@tenniseurope.org with the entry form.

**Q: I was removed from a tournament I did not withdraw from. Why?**
A: The system automatically withdraws players from lower-priority events when they are accepted into multiple tournaments. This is by design.

**Q: Can my 12&Under child enter more than 10 tournaments per year?**
A: No. 12&Under players are limited to 10 tournaments per year (max 1 per week), not counting team events.

**Q: Do Summer Cups count toward the annual tournament limit?**
A: No. Team events (Summer Cups, Winter Cups) are excluded from the annual tournament count.

## Rankings Questions

**Q: Why does my child have no ranking?**
A: If they are in the 12&Under age group, this is normal. 12&Under players do not have individual rankings. They receive Organiser Acceptances (OA) for all draws.

**Q: Why did my ranking drop?**
A: Tennis Europe uses a 52-week rolling ranking. Results older than 52 weeks drop off automatically.

**Q: What counts toward my ranking?**
A: Best 6 singles results + best 2 doubles results + best 2 team event results over the past 52 weeks.
