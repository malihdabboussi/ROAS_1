# How a Junior Player Joins and Competes on the Tennis Europe Junior Tour

## Step 1: Get an IPIN
- Go to itftennis.com
- Provide name, date of birth, email
- Verify identity, activate via email link, set password
- Pay annual fee: US$60 for juniors (valid 12 months, non-refundable)
- One IPIN per player. Renew via: My Profile > Circuits > Renew Membership
- Players only in 12/14/16&Under events do NOT need ITF online courses

## Step 2: Create a Tennis Europe Account
- Go to: tenniseurope.org/member/createaccount.aspx
- Used to enter tournaments, manage priority lists, and track results

## Step 3: Find and Enter Tournaments
- Browse the tournament calendar on tenniseurope.org
- Enter online (preferred) or via email to juniors@tenniseurope.org
- Entry and Withdrawal deadlines strictly enforced
- Automatic withdrawals apply from lower-priority events if accepted into multiple tournaments

## Step 4: Sign In and Compete
- Sign-in is mandatory at all events
- One entry fee covers both singles and doubles

## Age Group Rules
| Age Group | Max Tournaments/Year | Has Rankings | Category |
|-----------|---------------------|--------------|----------|
| 12 & Under | 10 (excl. team events), max 1/week | No | Cat 3 only |
| 14 & Under | No limit | Yes | Cat 1, 2, 3 |
| 16 & Under | No limit | Yes | Cat 1, 2, 3 |
| 18 & Under | No limit | Yes | Cat 1, 2, 3 |

## Entry Fees (2025+)
- Up to 400 EUR per team for major events
- 120 EUR per player for 14/16/18&Under Championships
