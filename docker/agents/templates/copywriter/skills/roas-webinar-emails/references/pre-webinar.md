# Pre-Webinar Sequence — Fill the Room

Three jobs on the front end, in order: get registrations (reactivation, if there's an existing list to mine), confirm the registrant and set the frame, then maximize show-up rate with a day-of reminder stack. Every email pairs with an SMS. See `assets/swipe-pre-webinar.md` for the proven version.

Show-up sits around 30-40% of registrants for cold traffic, higher for warm/list. The day-of stack and the SMS pairing move that number more than anything else.

---

## PART A — REACTIVATION (only if there's an existing/aged list to drive registrations from)

Runs the day before and morning of, to a list that already knows the presenter. Three emails + paired SMS. The job is registrations, using the angles in `references/angles.md`:

1. **"They told me not to do this"** — pattern interrupt. The presenter is doing something their team thinks is crazy (giving away the $X system free, one time). Frames the webinar as a rare event. Register link + lead magnet in the P.S.
2. **"It's not the tech, it's not the time"** — names the reader's excuses, reframes the real problem as a missing system, bridges to the training. P.S.: "this goes to the cold list tomorrow, register while the room is small."
3. **"I'll be honest with you"** — the aged-list call-out. Acknowledges they've been on the list a while and never moved, without judgment. The people who launched weren't more ready, they just stopped waiting. Morning-of, ~4 hours before.

Skip Part A entirely for pure paid-traffic registrations with no list behind them.

---

## PART B — CONFIRMATION (fires instantly on registration) — CORE

The most-opened email in the funnel. They just raised their hand.

- **The 2-step confirm mechanic**: "you're registered... kinda. I need two quick things." [1] Whitelist / add to contacts (protects deliverability for the whole sequence). [2] Click the confirm page to add to calendar + grab the join link. Asking for a small action raises commitment and show-up.
- Date, time, **timezone**, how to attend.
- Tease the 3-4 things they'll learn (build anticipation for the teaching beats).
- **Live-only reasons to show**: bonuses for live attendees + a Q&A framed as a free group coaching session. State these here and again in the reminders.
- **A lead magnet on the confirmation page** (free guide / blueprint) over-delivers before the event and gives a reason to click through.
- Two paired SMS: one instant confirmation ("got you locked in for [date/time]"), one shortly after pointing to the confirm page and the bonus on it.
- Subject angle: "you're in (kinda), read this first" — the parenthetical creates the open.

---

## PART C — DAY-OF REMINDER STACK — CORE

Escalating brevity as it counts down. Each email paired with an SMS from the 1-hour mark on.

- **4 hours before** — "here's exactly what you're walking into." Walk the 5 things they'll see, with named case studies and numbers. "Bring something to write with." Builds the value frame right before go-time.
- **1 hour before** — "1 hour." Short. The single biggest promise + link. SMS paired.
- **15 minutes before** — "starting in 15... here's your link." Just the join link. SMS paired.
- **Live now (5 min after start)** — the re-click trick. "Some people had issues with the link so I reset it on Zoom. Try this one: [link]. We already started, hop in." This legitimate-sounding reset recovers people who ignored every earlier reminder and is the highest-leverage pre-webinar send. SMS paired.

---

## Rules specific to pre-webinar

- **Pair every reminder with an SMS** from the confirmation onward. SMS open rates dwarf email; the room fills on the texts as much as the emails.
- **Timezone on every send.** "2pm EST / 11am PST."
- **Live-only reason to attend** in the confirmation and again in the hour-before and live sends.
- **Escalating brevity.** Confirmation and reactivation can breathe. By "15 minutes" and "live now" it's two lines and a link.
- **One promise per reminder**, not the full 3-secret stack. The teaching beats live in the confirmation; the reminders ride the single biggest outcome.
- **Don't sell the offer here.** The only thing being sold pre-webinar is showing up live.
