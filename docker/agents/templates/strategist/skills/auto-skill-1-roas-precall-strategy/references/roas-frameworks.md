# ROAS Internal Frameworks — Condensed Reference

Source docs: "WHAT IS AN OFFER," "Designing A Compelling & Scalable Offer," "ROAS SUCCESSFUL CLIENT AVATAR," "TEMPLATE - Market Research Process (Long)." These are the frameworks the strategy map thinks with.

## 1. Offer Structure (WHAT IS AN OFFER)

An offer is a packaged solution: what they get, what result it creates, why it's the fastest/easiest/safest path. Five parts:

1. **Product** — the one-stop-shop, stacked into 3-5 named components. Target reaction to the stack: "this is a LOT."
2. **Price** — anchored (vs. hiring, DIY cost, competitors, cost of failing), then dropped. Example pattern: $25K hire → $15K DIY year → $8K tools → $5K → $1,997 today.
3. **Bonuses** — each must kill an objection, add speed, simplicity, certainty, or urgency.
4. **Risk reversal** — a NAMED aggressive guarantee ("Better Than Money Back": don't 2x your investment, get double back).
5. **Scarcity** — real only: limited seats, expiring bonus, price rise, application-only, 24-hour link.

## 2. Compelling & Scalable Offer (3S + mechanism)

- A scalable offer works on **cold traffic** and survives **2-5x fulfillment volume** (path to $3-5M/yr).
- **3S formula:** solves a specific problem, for a specific person, a specific way (unique mechanism). Specificity is what makes cold traffic convert and premium pricing stick.
- **You cannot create desire, only channel it** (Schwartz). Put the offer in front of existing desire or channel adjacent desire into it.
- **Evolutionary beats revolutionary:** model what's already working, add ONE distinct advantage (IP, fulfillment, AOV/LTV, acquisition channel, method, demographic, psychographic).
- **Mechanism script:** "Most people trying to get [result] do [common approach]. Here's the problem with that... So instead we [methodology], which allows [benefit], which ultimately means [benefit of benefit]." The mechanism simultaneously explains why past attempts failed and why this will be different.
- **Market test (Hormozi):** value you can provide (trumps all), growing/neutral market, clear problem, findable audience, purchasing power, TAM ≥ 30k.
- **Pricing benchmarks** (90-150 day terms max, 3-5 payments max): Health $2,800-3,800 → premium $5,800-8,800. Relationships $3,800 → $5,800-7,800. Biz-opp $4,800 → $9,800. B2-solopreneur $5,800 → $9,800-12K. B2B $5,800-48K+. Charge ~1/10th of delivered value. Raise price when day-1 cash >40%, AR collected >80%, close rate >20-25%.

## 3. Winning Client Traits (SUCCESSFUL CLIENT AVATAR)

The Perfect Client: coaches, consultants, speakers, niche experts with a proven offer — **$10K+/month already**, defined niche, clear transformation, proof on hand (testimonials/screenshots/video), good or willing on camera, coachable and decisive. Scale mode, not idea mode.

11 traits to score against:
1. Proof of concept (making money organically pre-ads)
2. Defined avatar + crystal-clear offer with a "New Way" mechanism
3. Strong organic foundation (list size, followers, engagement)
4. Confident + follows instructions (deviators flop)
5. Webinar format fits (live 60-90 min, pitch at 45-60 min)
6. SMS + Email + AddEvent show-up system acceptance
7. Willing to dial slides/offer with us (done-with-you sessions)
8. Bold on camera, runs own sales calls
9. Strong visual identity (or willing to build the launch kit)
10. Judges on 90-day LTV, not night-of revenue
11. Accepts simple, consistent ad formula

Every trait miss = a risk line in the map with a mitigation.

## 4. Market Research Process (condensed)

- **Competitors (3):** positioning, deliverables, price, terms, bonuses, guarantees, headline themes, USP, claims, mechanisms, what their testimonials praise, top ads. Goal: build FAR SUPERIOR and counter-position.
- **Customer profile (SMDQ):** what keeps them up at night, what they fear, who they're angry at, top 3 daily frustrations, secret desire, decision bias, their jargon, who else sells to them. Plus: what lies are they believing and why.
- **Awareness levels:** Most Aware → Product → Solution → Problem → Unaware. Colder = lead with benefit/presell; dead market = revive with a new mechanism.
- **Sophistication stages:** (1) first mover: direct claim. (2) inflate claims. (3) market heard it all: sell the mechanism, not the benefit. (4) elaborate the mechanism. (5) dead: identification copy.
- **Unique mechanism questions:** What component uniquely delivers the result? What common component is nobody talking about?

## 5. What "good" looks like (the 10-line bar)

1. Specific problem, specific person, specific way — or cold traffic won't convert.
2. Channels existing desire; evolutionary with one clear advantage.
3. One-stop-shop stack, anchored price, named guarantee, real scarcity.
4. Fulfillment survives 2-5x; TAM ≥ 30k; growing or neutral market.
5. Priced ~1/10th of value, at/above competitors, stair-stepped later.
6. Grounded in real competitor + customer research, matched to awareness/sophistication.
7. Client at $10K+/mo with organic proof — scale mode, not idea mode.
8. Defined niche, "New Way" mechanism, proof assets, existing audience.
9. Coachable, bold on camera, sales-capable, follows the plan.
10. Flagship vehicle: webinar launch stack — 60-90 min live, pitch at 45-60, show-up system, 72-hour follow-up, backend ladder judged on 90-day LTV.
