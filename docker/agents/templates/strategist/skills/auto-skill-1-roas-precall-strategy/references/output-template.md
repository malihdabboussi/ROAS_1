## Artifact 2: Confirm-or-Correct Call Agenda

```
# [Client] — Onboarding Call Agenda ([date])
Frame to open with: "We've already mapped your business. We're going to walk
you through what we think, and you tell us where we're wrong."

## Confirm or correct (replaces discovery — guided steps 2-4)
1. [Checkable statement] → if wrong: [fallback]
2. [Checkable statement] → if wrong: [fallback]
[5-8 items total, ordered by how much the strategy changes if wrong]

## Fill the gaps
[The [MISSING] items phrased as direct questions]

## Access + logistics (guided step 5 — unchanged)
[Standard: Meta assets, creative assets, email list, GHL status]

## Must leave the call verified
Offer · pricing · campaign type we're running for sure · budget

## Wrap-up
- Ground rules: 48-hour rule, date ownership, Monday update cadence.
- Tell the client: "You'll have our full strategy in your Slack channel by end
  of day." (No strategy session call — auto-skill-2-roas-strategy-adjust + strategist
  approval by EOD.)
```

## Routing (every run)

1. Save both artifacts in the client's Drive folder.
2. Link the map in the portal Strategist tab (strategy brief link field).
3. Send the agenda to whoever runs the call.
4. After the call: run auto-skill-2-roas-strategy-adjust (map + transcript + AM notes →
   final strategy + client message). Strategist approves by EOD. That approved
   doc is the strategy artifact downstream skills build from.
